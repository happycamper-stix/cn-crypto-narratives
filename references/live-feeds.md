What to watch for: a term is *forming* when multiple mid-size CN accounts use it unglossed within 24–48h, and *formed* when BlockBeats/Odaily newsflashes use it in headlines.

## Telegram / WeChat propagation

- **Telegram**: alpha-call channels and 打狗/冲土狗 groups are where launchpad tokens get named and pumped; media channels (above) then formalize the vocabulary. Search Telegram for 中文 + 土狗/金狗/内盘 to find the current crop — channels churn constantly, so track the *pattern* not specific channels.
- **WeChat**: closed groups (微信群) are the original propagation layer (per the 2018 Quartz piece in [[archived-glossaries]]) — invitation-only, unindexable. Public-facing proxy: WeChat 公众号 articles that recap group chatter; these surface on sogou WeChat search (weixin.sogou.com).
- Propagation path is typically: WeChat/TG insider group → CT KOL tweet → BlockBeats 快讯 → PANews/TechFlow explainer → EN media 1–2 weeks later.

## On-chain / launchpad surfaces for Chinese-named tokens

- **Four.meme** — https://four.meme — BNB Chain launchpad, epicenter of the Chinese-language meme wave (币安人生 etc.); scan new launches for hanzi/pinyin names
- **pump.fun** — https://pump.fun — Solana; search hanzi strings or pinyin tickers in the token list
- **GMGN** — https://gmgn.ai — CN-community-favored screener/sniping tool; its UI and trending tabs are themselves a window into what CN degens chase (interface bilingual)
- **DEX Screener** — https://dexscreener.com — filter new pairs by chain (BSC for CN metas), search by hanzi in token-name search
- Tell for a CN-origin meta: cluster of new BSC tokens whose names are Chinese puns/homophones within hours of a CZ or He Yi tweet.
