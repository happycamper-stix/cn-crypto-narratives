# Archived Glossaries

Full term extractions from the two flagged sources in [[sources]]. The live Quartz URL 403s; retrieved via Wayback Machine. The Xuehua URL is dead in both live web and Wayback, but the article turns out to be a Chinese translation/summary of the same Quartz piece ("老外眼中" = "in foreigners' eyes"), and mirrors survive — extracted from the Sohu mirror below.

## Source 1 — Quartz (retrieved ✅)

**"Leek, candy, and big pancake: The slang Chinese crypto traders use to hide from the authorities"**
By Zheping Huang, Quartz, April 30, 2018.
Snapshot: `https://web.archive.org/web/20181012141344/https://qz.com/1265459/why-chinas-crypto-traders-call-bitcoin-big-pancake-and-other-secrets-of-local-slang/`

Framing: slang emerged partly to obscure trading talk from authorities after the Sept 2017 exchange crackdown; trading moved to WeChat/Telegram P2P and forums like 8btc.com.

### Investors
| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 币圈 | bì quān | coin circle | The crypto space/community |
| 大佬 | dà lǎo | boss | Big trader; Western "whale" |
| 狗庄 | gǒu zhuāng | dog banker | Market-moving manipulators; from gambling/stock parlance, used in retail complaints |
| 韭菜 | jiǔcài | leek | Newbie investors who get "harvested"; they grow back as new naive money arrives |
| 老韭菜 | lǎo jiǔcài | old leek | Longtime retail investor repeatedly burned by whales |
| 币妈 | bì mā | coin mom | Short for 币圈大妈, "coin-circle aunties" — middle-aged women chasing new assets without investment skills; lineage: 2013 gold rush → 2015 stock bubble → crypto |

### Transactions
| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 囤币 | tún bì | hoarding coins | Stay invested, keep accumulating; "hodl" |
| 糖果 | tángguǒ | candy | Free token giveaways for signing up to ICO projects; "airdrop" |
| 薅羊毛 | hāo yángmáo | pulling out the wool | Bonus-hunting across banks/platforms; airdrop farming is one form |
| 割韭菜 | gē jiǔcài | cutting leeks | Harvesting retail investors |
| 搬砖 | bān zhuān | moving bricks | Arbitrage across markets (e.g. buy in Japan, sell at a premium in banned China) |
| 梭哈 | suōhā | Show Hand (poker) | All in; transliteration from HK gambling films. Meme: "梭哈，赢了会所嫩模，输了下海干活" ("win: young models at clubs; lose: farm work") |

## Source 2 — Xuehua 大妈、韭菜、狗庄 (retrieved via mirror ✅)

**「大妈、韭菜、狗庄：老外眼中的中文币圈俚语大全」** — Chinese translation of the Quartz article. Original xuehua.us URL dead and not in Wayback; surviving mirrors:
- Sohu (byline 网汇贷理财, 2018-05-10): https://www.sohu.com/a/231129607_507882
- read01: https://read01.com/zh-cn/nx2xm3e.html
- ChainNews: https://www.chainnews.com/articles/265479916039.htm

Terms covered (identical set to Quartz investors + transactions sections): 币圈, 大佬, 狗庄, 韭菜, 老韭菜, 币妈, 囤币, 糖果, 薅羊毛, 割韭菜, 搬砖, 梭哈. Notable additions in the Chinese version:
- 老韭菜 is glossed positively as "经受过大起大落考验并活下来的投资者" (survivor framing), softer than Quartz's "routinely gets burned".
- The full 梭哈 meme is quoted verbatim: 「梭哈，赢了会所嫩模，输了下海干活」.
- The coin-nickname table was **dropped** in translation — the mirror only notes "每个主要的加密币都有一个外号…主要以听起来类似的食物命名" without listing them. Quartz remains the canonical source for the food-name table.

**Conclusion:** the two flagged sources are one glossary, not two. No net-new terms in Xuehua beyond the Quartz set.
