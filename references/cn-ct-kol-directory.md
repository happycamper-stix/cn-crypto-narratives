# Chinese CT KOL Directory

A field guide to the famous accounts of Chinese-language crypto Twitter/X (中文区 CT). Compiled 2026-08 from ChainCatcher, Biteye/XHunt, Feixiaohao, Bitget Academy, BlockWeeks, and Odaily coverage (links in Sources).

## How to use this list

- **This is a decoding aid, not a follow endorsement.** The CN KOL economy runs on 喊单/带单 monetization (see [[kol-shilling-ecosystem]]) — the default assumption inside the community itself is that any account with reach is monetizing it. "Known for / caveats" flags what coverage has reported; absence of a flag is not a clean bill of health.
- **Follower counts are approximate snapshots** (mostly from 2025 ranking articles) and marked (?) where unverified. Handles rotate after bans/rebrands — reverify before citing.
- **Read it with [[live-feeds]]**: media accounts here are the formalization layer; KOL accounts are the narrative-formation layer one step upstream.
- **Rankings to re-pull for freshness**: ChainCatcher's annual KOL lists, the MetaEra × Biteye × XHunt 中文KOL影响力榜 (kol.xhunt.ai), and Biteye's 华语加密KOL影响力图鉴 report.

## KOL-specific lingo

- **KOL轮** ("KOL round") — projects allocate discounted tokens to dozens of KOLs instead of a marketing budget; followers buy the KOL's unlock. The structural reason "research threads" are ads.
- **喊单 / 带单 / 跟单** — shouting a call / leading trades in a paid group / copying the call. **老师 (teacher)** culture imports tutoring-deference into gambling; 带单老师 is often the counterparty.
- **接广 / 恰饭** — taking sponsored posts ("eating"). 研报-style threads (pseudo research reports) are the standard ad format.
- **割粉丝 / 翻车** — dumping on your own followers / a call blowing up publicly. Some KOLs post 翻车实录 (crash post-mortems) as a trust-building performance.
- **纯干货 / 清流** — "pure substance / clean stream": the minority tier of analysis-only accounts, rank-listed precisely because everyone else is assumed compromised.
- **科学家** — "scientist": scripted multi-wallet airdrop farmer; the elite tier of the 撸毛 world ([[lumao-industrial-complex]]).
- **内部群 / 财富密码** — paid "insider group" sold with "wealth code" language; the real funnel behind most free content.
