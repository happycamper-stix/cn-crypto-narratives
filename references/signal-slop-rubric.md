# Signal-vs-Slop Rubric

## Context
Scoring rubric for evaluating a Chinese-CT X post: is it narrative signal worth
decoding, or 喊单/engagement slop? Used by the Telegram bot's /analyze feature and
by hand. Grounded in [[kol-shilling-ecosystem]] and [[cn-ct-kol-directory]].

## Signal markers (raise score)
- **Specific, checkable claims**: named contracts/addresses, dated events, on-chain
  numbers that can be verified (0xSun-style receipts culture).
- **Narrative mechanics explained**, not just asserted — the post tells you *why*
  a name/meta works (homophone, 板块 rotation logic, listing pathway).
- **Term precision**: correct native use of register-appropriate slang (板块/龙头
  rotation grammar, 埋伏 with an actual thesis, 铭文-era vocabulary in context).
- **Costly stance**: admits losses (关灯吃面 self-report), positions against the
  crowd, or documents an exit — slop never publishes its losses.
- **Original information**: first-hand screenshots WITH verifiable on-chain echo,
  early naming of a meta that later chart data confirms.

## Slop markers (lower score)
- **喊单 grammar**: 冲/梭哈/一把梭 imperatives with no thesis; 目标价 + rocket
  emojis; "最后上车机会" (last chance to board) urgency.
- **带单 funnel moves**: 私信/进群/VX contact bait, 内部消息 claims, paid-group
  screenshots of PnL (P图 risk — see [[emoji-sticker-visual-codes]] on the
  screenshot economy).
- **KOL轮 tells**: relentless single-project promotion at listing/unlock windows —
  the shiller's allocation IS the exit (see [[kol-shilling-ecosystem]]).
- **财富密码 formula**: "改变命运" destiny-ticket framing, 一币一别墅 promises,
  guaranteed-x multipliers.
- **Engagement anomalies**: reply/like ratios inconsistent with follower count,
  reply sections full of identical 冲冲冲 bot spam.
- **Flagged author**: handle appears in [[cn-ct-kol-directory]] with reputational
  flags (400u 战队 cluster, documented 割粉丝 incidents).

## Price-vs-narrative checks
- Post BEFORE the move with a mechanism stated = potential signal; post AFTER the
  move narrating it = content, not alpha (牛回速归 genre).
- 埋伏 calls on tokens with < $50k liquidity are self-fulfilling pump mechanics,
  not analysis — the caller IS the catalyst and the exit.
- Fresh pair (< 7 days) + blessing-formula name + KOL push = the
  [[launchpad-naming-meta]] attention-asset pattern; expect volcanic decay
  (火山喷发式 — see [[meme-season-aftermath]]).

## Verdict bands
- **8–10 signal**: checkable, mechanistic, costly-stance content.
- **4–7 mixed**: real narrative reading wrapped in promotion; extract the read,
  ignore the call.
- **0–3 slop**: 喊单/带单/财富密码 formula; useful only as sentiment data —
  heavy slop volume on a theme is itself a top signal (see [[irony-cope-register]]).
