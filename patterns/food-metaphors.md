# Food & Animal Metaphors

## Context
Chinese crypto slang leans heavily on food and animal imagery to describe market
roles and assets. The metaphors form a coherent little ecosystem: retail are crops,
operators are farmers/butchers, coins are food items.

## The system
- **韭菜 (jiǔcài, chives/leeks)** — retail investors, because chives regrow after
  cutting: an endlessly harvestable crowd. Root of [[ge-jiu-cai]] (割韭菜, "cutting
  leeks" = dumping on retail) and 老韭菜 ("old leek", a veteran who still loses).
- **大饼 (dàbǐng, big flatbread)** — BTC. "Eating the flatbread" = holding BTC.
  ETH follows as 二饼 ("second bread") — see [[da-bing]].
- **土狗 (tǔgǒu, dirt dog / mongrel)** — low-quality local memecoin, degen shitcoin.
- **巨鲸 (jùjīng, giant whale)** — whale wallets (parallel to English usage).
- **羊毛 (yángmáo, wool)** — freebies/airdrops; 撸羊毛 "pulling wool" = airdrop
  farming; 羊毛党 = the farming crowd.
- **抬轿子 (carrying the sedan chair)** — buying to lift someone else's exit.

## Decoding heuristic
If a term names a food, crop, or animal, ask which market role it maps to in the
harvest metaphor: who grows, who cuts, who gets eaten.
