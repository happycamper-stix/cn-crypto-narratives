# Suffix Morphology (后缀构词)

## Context
Chinese builds crypto vocabulary by snapping productive suffixes onto a root — a
one-character classifier that tells you instantly what *kind* of thing (or person)
is being discussed. Learn the half-dozen suffixes and you can parse terms you've
never seen.

## Mechanics
- **-币 (bì, "coin")** — the default coin-former: 狗狗币 (Doge), 屎币 (SHIB,
  "shit-coin" as its actual Chinese name), 空气币 ("air coin" = vaporware token),
  山寨币 ("mountain-stronghold/knockoff coin" = altcoin), 主流币 (majors),
  稳定币 (stablecoin), 土狗币 (degen memecoin).
- **-盘 (pán, "plate/scheme")** — marks a *structure of money flow*, usually
  fraudulent: 资金盘 ("funds scheme" = ponzi), 传销盘 (MLM scheme), 杀猪盘
  (pig-butchering scam), 土狗盘 (rug-pull memecoin operation), 接盘 ("receive the
  plate" = bagholding someone's exit), 开盘/崩盘 (launch/collapse). If it ends in
  盘, someone is being farmed.
- **-圈 (quān, "circle")** — a scene/subculture: 币圈 (coin circle = crypto
  trading world), 链圈 (chain circle = blockchain-tech people, who look down on
  币圈), 矿圈 (miners), 盘圈 (ponzi-scheme world). The four circles are a folk
  taxonomy of the whole industry with a built-in status hierarchy.
- **-党 (dǎng, "faction/party")** — people defined by one behavior: 羊毛党
  (airdrop/bonus farmers), 屯币党 (hodler faction), 空气币党, 打新党 (new-listing
  snipers). Mildly derogatory, like English "-maxi" crossed with "gang".
- **-侠 (xiá, "knight-errant")** — ironic wuxia honorific: 亏损侠 ("loss knight"),
  抄底侠 ("bottom-fishing knight" — always early, always wrong). Mock-heroic
  framing of trading behaviors.
- **-神 (shén, "god")** — genuine or ironic mastery: 币圈大神 (trading god), 赌神
  (gambling god, via HK film — see [[gambling-film-register]]), and the YYDS
  formula 永远的神.
- **-王 (wáng, "king")** — 套牢王 (king of being trapped in a position), 梭哈王.
- **割 (gē, "to cut") as a root** — productive on the verb side: 割韭菜 (harvest
  retail), 被割 (get cut), 割肉 ("cut flesh" = sell at a loss), 互割 (communities
  scalping each other). See [[ge-jiu-cai]].

## Decoding heuristic
Parse unknown terms right-to-left: the last character usually classifies
(coin? scheme? scene? faction? person?), and the preceding characters describe.
盘 = assume scam; 圈 = a tribe; 党 = a behavior-defined crowd; 侠/神/王 = a
(usually ironic) title.
