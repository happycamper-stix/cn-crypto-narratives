# Classical & Literary Register (文言/典故借用)

## Context
Chinese crypto naming reaches for archaic, literary, and mystical vocabulary to
lend gravitas (or ironic mock-gravitas) to new tech. Translators of protocol
names deliberately pick characters with classical resonance, and traders quote
chengyu (four-character idioms) as market commentary.

## Mechanics
- **铭文 (míngwén, "inscription")** — the chosen translation for Bitcoin Ordinals
  "inscriptions". 铭 is the word for text engraved on ancient bronze vessels and
  stone steles; it frames minting JSON onto satoshis as carving on ritual bronzes.
  The whole 2023–24 mania was 铭文季, "inscription season".
- **符文 (fúwén, "rune")** — for the Runes protocol; 符 is the Daoist talisman
  character (符咒, spell-papers), so "runes" arrive in Chinese pre-loaded with
  native magic rather than Norse.
- **坊 (fáng)** in 以太坊 — Ethereum's translator rejected pure transliteration
  and chose 坊, the character for a traditional workshop/quarter (作坊, 街坊):
  Ethereum as a crafts district where you build things. A deliberate literary
  choice that shaped how Chinese users conceptualize the chain. See
  [[transliteration-mechanics]].
- **道 / 玄学 framing** — trading wisdom is phrased as Daoist cultivation: 悟道
  (attain enlightenment = finally get it), 玄学 ("mysticism" = picking coins by
  vibes/numerology), 佛系持币 ("Buddhist-style holding" = detached hodling).
- **Chengyu as chart commentary** — 一泻千里 ("a torrent plunging a thousand li"
  = freefall), 高山流水 (ironically: from mountain peak to flowing water),
  守株待兔 ("guarding the stump waiting for rabbits" = waiting for a repeat pump),
  刻舟求剑 (marking the boat to find a dropped sword = using stale price levels).
- **老子/古人 quotation** — mock-classical pronouncements ("古人云…") deployed for
  comic authority before terrible financial advice.

## Decoding heuristic
A crypto term written in suspiciously elegant or archaic characters is doing
register work: it borrows the authority of bronze inscriptions, Daoist talismans,
or classical idiom to make speculation feel like cultivation. Ask what the
classical source object is — that's the intended aura.
