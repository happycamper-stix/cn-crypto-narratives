# Gambling-Film Register (赌片黑话)

## Context
A whole layer of Chinese crypto slang comes not from finance but from Hong Kong
gambling cinema of the late 80s–90s — the 赌神 (*God of Gamblers*, 1989, Chow
Yun-fat) franchise and its imitators. These films taught a generation the
vocabulary of the casino table, and crypto (openly called 赌场, "the casino")
adopted it wholesale.

## Mechanics
- **梭哈 (suōhā)** — transliteration of "show hand", the five-card-stud variant
  featured in HK gambling films; in crypto = go all-in. Often shortened to 梭
  ("梭了!" = "I aped everything"), or intensified: 卖房梭哈 ("sell the house and
  all-in"). The English word never appears — the *film* word does.
- **庄家 (zhuāngjiā, "the banker/house")** — the market maker / project insiders.
  Retail vs 庄 is the core adversarial frame: 庄 controls the deck. 坐庄 = to run
  the table (manipulate a coin); 控盘 = deck-stacking.
- **赌神 / 赌怪** — "gambling god", honorific for traders with absurd win streaks
  (see [[suffix-morphology]] on -神).
- **All-in culture verbs**: 下注 (place bets), 加注 (raise), 补仓 as "rebuy" —
  position management described as poker staking.
- **一把梭 (yì bǎ suō)** — "one hand, all-in": the meme phrase from the 2018
  "区块链一把梭" viral image (a betting-parlor style slogan), which became the
  self-mocking motto of ICO-era degens.
- **十赌九输** — "of ten gambles, nine lose": the proverb quoted at every rug,
  acknowledging the casino frame while continuing to play. Cope — see
  [[irony-cope-register]].

## Decoding heuristic
When a Chinese trading term feels like it belongs at a card table, read the trade
through casino roles: 庄 = whoever controls supply, 闲家/散户 = retail punters,
梭哈 = all-in. The register signals the speaker *knows* it's gambling — that
self-awareness is part of the meaning.
