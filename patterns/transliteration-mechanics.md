# Transliteration Mechanics (音译 vs 意译 vs 音意兼译)

## Context
Every English coin name entering Chinese passes through a translation choice, and
the choice is never neutral: each character selected carries its own meaning, so
a "phonetic" rendering silently editorializes. Three strategies exist, and the
best names hit sound *and* meaning at once (音意兼译) — the strategy behind
Coca-Cola 可口可乐 and, in crypto, 币安.

## Mechanics
- **Sound-only (音译)**: 比特币 (bǐtè-bì, Bitcoin — 比特 was already the CS term
  for "bit"), 波卡 (bōkǎ, Polkadot), 索拉纳 (Solana), 艾达 (ADA). Characters
  chosen mainly for neutrality.
- **Meaning-only (意译)**: 屎币 ("shit coin") for SHIB — abandoning the sound
  entirely for the joke; 太阳币 rarely for SUN-type names.
- **Sound + meaning (音意兼译)** — the prestige strategy:
  - **币安 (Bì'ān, Binance)** — sounds like "Binance", reads as "coin-safety".
    A trust claim smuggled into the phonetics.
  - **以太坊 (Yǐtàifáng, Ethereum)** — 以太 is the established physics rendering
    of "ether" (the same 以太 as in 以太网, Ethernet), plus 坊 "workshop" chosen
    by the whitepaper's Chinese translators 咕噜 and 巨蟹, who by their own
    account rejected 以太工厂 ("ether factory") for the -eum suffix (per 界面新闻,
    《以太坊简史》); the name argues Ethereum is a place where you build. NB 坊
    has two readings: fáng = workshop (作坊) — the coiners' intended morpheme —
    vs fāng = quarter/lane (街坊); both circulate in speech (Wiktionary records
    Mainland fáng, Taiwan fāng). See [[classical-literary-register]].
  - **瑞波 (Ruìbō, Ripple)** — "auspicious wave": sound-alike plus luck character
    瑞, a classic lucky-character insertion.
  - **莱特币 (Láitè-bì, Litecoin)** — 莱特 is neutral-phonetic, but folk
    etymology reads 莱 fields as "lite/light".
- **Degradation into nickname**: official transliterations get worn down into
  homophone jokes — 以太 → 姨太 ("concubine", see [[da-yi-tai]]), 索拉纳 → 索拉拉
  (cutified, see [[reduplication-cuteness]]), Solana → 傻拉纳 (mocking, 傻 =
  stupid) when it's down. The nickname layer tracks community sentiment in real
  time.
- **Reverse direction (2025+)**: Chinese-native names now go *outward*
  untranslated — 币安人生 ("Binance Life"), 哈基米 — with Latin tickers derived
  from pinyin, forcing English speakers to do the decoding for once. See
  [[launchpad-naming-meta]].

## Decoding heuristic
For any Chinese coin name: (1) say it aloud — does it approximate the English?
(2) read each character's meaning — is there a smuggled claim (safety, luck,
craft)? (3) check whether the form you're seeing is the official rendering or a
worn-down homophone nickname, because the nickname carries the sentiment.
