# A-Share Inheritance (A股血统)

## Context
The single largest donor language of Chinese crypto slang is not English CT and
not gaming — it is **A股** (A-gǔ, mainland A-share stock market) retail culture.
Half the vault's entries carry a passing "from A-shares" aside; this note is the
dedicated mechanics: *why* the pipeline exists, *which* terms predate crypto
(with birth certificates where findable), *what connotations* they smuggle in,
and *where the mapping silently breaks*. The companion hazard note —
mandatory reading before decoding any color word — is [[red-green-inversion]].

## Why the pipeline exists
1. **Demographics.** The first crypto cohorts (2013 大妈 era, 2017 ICO wave)
   were 股民 (gǔmín, "stock citizens" = stock retail) first. The framing verb
   炒币 (chǎo bì, "stir-fry coins" = speculate in crypto) is directly cloned
   from 炒股/炒房 — crypto entered Chinese as *one more 炒 asset class*
   ([[cn-crypto-eras-timeline]]).
2. **Media formats.** CN crypto media (Odaily, PANews, BlockBeats) clone
   stock-media genres — 快讯 (kuàixùn, newsflash), 复盘 (fùpán, "replay the
   board" = post-session review), 研报 (research note) — and with the format
   comes the vocabulary.
3. **Structural rhyme.** A-shares are a retail-dominated, manipulation-heavy,
   narrative-driven market with weak shareholder protection. Crypto is the same
   machine minus the regulator. The A-share 庄家 model (see below) maps 1:1
   onto token 项目方 (project team) + 做市商 (market maker), so the whole
   manipulation lexicon transferred without modification.
4. **Scar-tissue import.** Most donor terms were minted in *scandals and
   crashes* (老鼠仓, 关灯吃面, 割韭菜). Adopting them imports a default
   worldview: the game is rigged, insiders win, retail's job is to be
   harvested. This is why CN crypto slang runs structurally more cynical and
   self-mocking than English CT's "gm/WAGMI" optimism — see
   [[irony-cope-register]].

## What the inheritance imports (connotations)
- **The 庄 assumption**: every chart has an operator. CN analysis asks 庄家想干嘛
  ("what does the banker want?") where English CT asks "what's the narrative?" —
  same chart, conspiracy-first vs story-first priors.
- **Loser-identity as membership**: 老韭菜 ("old leek") is a badge, 关灯吃面 a
  ritual. A-share culture had already normalized *losing as the shared retail
  experience*; crypto inherited the whole self-mocking register
  ([[irony-cope-register]]).
- **Crash literacy**: A-share memory (2007, 2015, 重庆啤酒-style single-stock
  disasters) supplies ready-made containers for crypto disasters — 519 slotted
  into a pre-existing genre of numbered catastrophe ([[number-slang]]).
- **Register clash with English CT**: translating 割韭菜 as "rug" loses that the
  Chinese term names a *recurring agricultural cycle*, not a one-off exit — the
  leek regrows and returns. The A-share layer is cyclical-fatalist where English
  scam slang is episodic.

## Where the mapping silently breaks
The verbs outlived the market microstructure that defined them. Decoders who
back-translate crypto usage into strict A-share mechanics will over-read:

| Term | A-share sense (mechanical) | Crypto shift |
|---|---|---|
| 打板 | buy into the 涨停 queue before it seals | just "chase the vertical candle" — no queue, no seal, no next-day 溢价 |
| 涨停/跌停 | hard ±10% daily bound (±20% on 科创板/创业板 boards) | pure hyperbole; the cliché 币圈没有涨停板 ("crypto has no limit-up board") is a standard risk warning — no floor either: 腰斩 (−50%) can happen in an hour |
| 打新 | regulated lottery with a historically near-guaranteed listing pop | launchpad/IDO punting where 破发 is routine — the term imports optimism the venue doesn't honor |
| 做T | workaround for T+1 settlement | unconstrained scalping; the "T" no longer refers to anything |
| 老鼠仓 | prosecutable crime (fines, lifetime bans) | on-chain-visible and near-universally unprosecuted; connotation drifts from *scandal* to *expected launch mechanics* |
| 停牌 (tíngpái, trading halt) | exchange suspends a stock | nothing to halt — the sarcastic crypto counterpart is 拔网线 (bá wǎngxiàn, "unplug the network cable"): exchanges "conveniently" going down during cascades |
| 收盘 (shōupán, market close) | daily close, 15:00 | no close exists — "币市的7×24小时交易，更让许多玩家感到心力交瘁" ("crypto's 7×24 trading leaves many players burned out"); the missing close is itself a named stressor in CN commentary |

## Decoding heuristic
When a CN crypto term smells institutional — built on 仓 (warehouse/position),
盘 (plate/board), 板 (board), 庄 (banker), 户 (household) — assume it named a
concrete A-share *mechanism*, then subtract the regulatory constraint: the
crypto meaning is usually the same gesture performed in a market without rules.
And route every color word (红/绿/阳/阴) through [[red-green-inversion]] before
translating.
