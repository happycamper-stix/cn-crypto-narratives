# Irony & Cope Register (自嘲/反讽)

## Context
Chinese crypto discourse runs on a thick layer of irony: terms that look sincere
are usually self-mocking, and the community's default self-image is the victim,
not the winner. Missing the irony inverts the meaning — the most dangerous
translation failure in this vault.

## Mechanics
- **韭菜 self-identification** — retail investors call *themselves* leeks
  (老韭菜, "old leek" = veteran who still gets cut; 新韭菜 = fresh meat). The
  harvest metaphor ([[food-metaphors]]) is worn as identity: "又被割了" ("got
  harvested again") posted with pride. Cf. English "exit liquidity" self-ID.
- **价值投资 (value investing), ironic** — said of memecoins and dead bags:
  "我这是价值投资" = "I'm down 90% but pretending it's Buffett-style conviction."
  Sincere only when about 大饼; ironic about everything else.
- **信仰 (xìnyǎng, "faith")** — holding as religion: 信仰充值 ("topping up
  faith" = good news arriving that justifies not selling), 信仰崩塌 (faith
  collapse). Half-sincere, half-mocking the fact that no rational thesis remains.
- **图一乐 (tú yí lè, "just for a laugh")** — the disclaimer verb: "买点图一乐"
  ("bought a bit just for fun") — insurance against looking stupid later, while
  secretly hoping for 100x.
- **一直涨 finger-pointing-up pose / 暴富 wishes** — 恭喜暴富 ("congrats on
  sudden wealth") as a *greeting*, ironizing the shared delusion; 财富自由
  ("financial freedom") almost always used ruefully.
- **归零 (guīlíng, "return to zero")** — matter-of-fact term for a coin dying;
  the deadpan "要归零了" is both warning and punchline.
- **接盘侠 (jiēpánxiá, "bag-receiving knight")** — the mock-heroic title for the
  last buyer; people volunteer as 接盘侠 jokingly. See [[suffix-morphology]].
- **十赌九输… 还有一平** — proverb-extension humor: "of ten gambles nine lose…
  and one breaks even", acknowledging the casino while re-entering it. See
  [[gambling-film-register]].
- **币圈一天，人间一年** — "a day in crypto, a year on earth": mock-immortal
  framing of volatility exhaustion. Riffs on the celestial-time trope 天上一日，
  地上一年; an intensified variant 币圈一天，人间十年 circulates just as widely,
  and both forms are attested in the wild (2018–2026) with no single canonical
  version.

## Decoding heuristic
Default to irony: when a Chinese crypto post uses sincere-sounding finance or
religious vocabulary (value investing, faith, freedom) about anything other than
BTC/ETH, read it as self-deprecating cope. Sincerity must be proven; mockery is
the unmarked case.
