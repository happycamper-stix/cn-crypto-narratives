# Narrative & Meta Terms (叙事黑话)

## Context
How Chinese crypto categorizes projects and narratives. The taxonomy of scams is
notably richer than English — the 盘 ("scheme") suffix family alone has several
members English lacks words for.

## Project taxonomy

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 山寨币 | shānzhàibì | mountain-stronghold coin | altcoin | 山寨 = bandit fortress → knockoff culture (山寨手机 fake phones); more pejorative than "altcoin" |
| 空气币 | kōngqìbì | air coin | vaporware token | stronger than shitcoin: nothing behind it but air |
| 传销币 | chuánxiāobì | MLM coin | ponzi/pyramid coin | 传销 is a loaded word in China (cult-like recruiting) |
| 国产币 | guóchǎnbì | domestic coin | Chinese-team projects | carries both patriotic-pump narrative and scam suspicion; nationalist framing common in 传销币 pitches |
| MEME币 / 土狗 | — | — | memecoin | 迷因币 is the Taiwanese/formal form |

## The 盘 (scheme) family

| Term | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 资金盘 | capital plate | ponzi (new money pays old) |
| 貔貅盘 | Pixiu scheme | honeypot — buy but never sell. 貔貅 is the mythical beast that eats gold and **has no anus**: money in, never out. The best zoological metaphor in crypto. |
| 杀猪盘 | pig-butchering scheme | romance-investment scam; victims are pigs "fattened" (养猪) before slaughter. Entered English as "pig butchering." |

## Meta / narrative vocabulary

| Term | Pinyin | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 铭文 | míngwén | inscriptions (Ordinals/BRC-20) | classical word for text engraved on bronze/stone — gave 2023 BRC-20 mania a "carving onto digital stone" resonance; 打铭文 = minting; CN retail dominated the meta |
| 符文 | fúwén | Runes protocol | 符 also evokes Daoist talismans |
| 信仰 | xìnyǎng | "faith" = conviction holding | 信仰充值 "recharging faith" = a pump that restores belief; religious framing of HODL |
| 价值投资 | jiàzhí tóuzī | "value investing" (ironic) | what you claim you're doing after your 土狗 dumps 90% and you can't exit |
| 牛回速归 | niú huí sù guī | "bull, return quickly" | prayer-meme; 猴市 "monkey market" = chop |
| 减半行情 | jiǎnbàn hángqíng | halving-narrative rally | |
| 币圈一天，人间十年 | — | "one day in crypto, ten years on earth" | the core compressed-time proverb; leverage variant 合约一天，人间一年 |
| 94 / 九四事件 | — | Sept 4 2017 PBoC ICO ban | dated trauma-reference every 老韭菜 knows; crashes get named like disasters (312, 519) |
| 图一乐 | tú yī lè | "just for a laugh" | the ironic-disclaimer stance for buying memecoins; 认真你就输了 "take it seriously and you lose" |
