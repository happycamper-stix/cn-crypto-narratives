# Narrative & Meta Terms (叙事黑话)

## Context
How Chinese crypto categorizes projects and narratives. The taxonomy of scams is
notably richer than English — the 盘 ("scheme") suffix family alone has several
members English lacks words for.

## Project taxonomy

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 山寨币 | shānzhàibì | mountain-stronghold coin | altcoin | 山寨 = bandit fortress → knockoff culture (山寨手机 fake phones); more pejorative than "altcoin" |
| 空气币 | kōngqìbì | air coin | vaporware token | stronger than shitcoin: nothing behind it but air |
| 传销币 | chuánxiāobì | MLM coin | ponzi/pyramid coin | 传销 is a loaded word in China (cult-like recruiting) |
| 国产币 | guóchǎnbì | domestic coin | Chinese-team projects | carries both patriotic-pump narrative and scam suspicion; nationalist framing common in 传销币 pitches |
| MEME币 / 土狗 | — | — | memecoin | 迷因币 is the Taiwanese/formal form |

## The 盘 (scheme) family

| Term | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 资金盘 | capital plate | ponzi (new money pays old) |
| 貔貅盘 | Pixiu scheme | honeypot — buy but never sell. 貔貅 is the mythical beast that eats gold and **has no anus**: money in, never out. The best zoological metaphor in crypto. |
| 杀猪盘 | pig-butchering scheme | romance-investment scam; victims are pigs "fattened" (养猪) before slaughter. Entered English as "pig butchering." |

## Meta / narrative vocabulary

| Term | Pinyin | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 铭文 | míngwén | inscriptions (Ordinals/BRC-20) | classical word for text engraved on bronze/stone — gave 2023 BRC-20 mania a "carving onto digital stone" resonance; 打铭文 = minting; CN retail dominated the meta |
| 符文 | fúwén | Runes protocol | 符 also evokes Daoist talismans |
| 信仰 | xìnyǎng | "faith" = conviction holding | 信仰充值 "recharging faith" = a pump that restores belief; religious framing of HODL |
| 价值投资 | jiàzhí tóuzī | "value investing" (ironic) | what you claim you're doing after your 土狗 dumps 90% and you can't exit |
| 牛回速归 | niú huí sù guī | "bull, return quickly" | prayer-meme; 猴市 "monkey market" = chop |
| 减半行情 | jiǎnbàn hángqíng | halving-narrative rally | |
| 币圈一天，人间十年 | — | "one day in crypto, ten years on earth" | the core compressed-time proverb; leverage variant 合约一天，人间一年 |
| 94 / 九四事件 | — | Sept 4 2017 PBoC ICO ban | dated trauma-reference every 老韭菜 knows; crashes get named like disasters (312, 519) |
| 图一乐 | tú yī lè | "just for a laugh" | the ironic-disclaimer stance for buying memecoins; 认真你就输了 "take it seriously and you lose" |

## DeFi / chain-era slang (链上黑话)

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 挖矿 / 流动性挖矿 | wākuàng | dig the mine | (yield) farming | agriculture became mining in CN framing; 挖提卖 "dig-withdraw-sell" = mercenary farm-and-dump loop |
| 头矿 | tóukuàng | head-of-mine | day-one farming APR | being first into a new farm, when emissions are absurd and the rug hasn't happened yet |
| 土矿 | tǔkuàng | dirt mine | no-name fork farm | 土 as in 土狗: an unaudited food-fork farm; you're there for 头矿 and pray you exit first |
| [[er-chi\|二池]] | èrchí | second pool | the token/native LP pool | see atomic note; where farms actually die |
| 无常损失 | wúcháng sǔnshī | impermanence loss | impermanent loss | 无常 is the Buddhist term for impermanence — LPs joke they're monks practicing 无常; punned as 无偿损失 "uncompensated loss" because it never comes back |
| 矿工费 / 燃料费 | kuànggōngfèi / ránliàofèi | miner fee / fuel fee | gas | usually just "gas"; 汽油费 also heard; complaining about ETH gas = 贵族链 "aristocrat chain" |
| 跨链 / 桥 | kuàliàn / qiáo | cross-chain / bridge | bridging | 桥又被黑了 "the bridge got hacked again" is its own genre of news |
| 撸空投 / 反撸 | lū kōngtóu / fǎnlū | stroke the airdrop / reverse-stroked | farm airdrops / get farmed by the project | 反撸 = your gas + time exceeded the drop: the project plucked *you*; 大毛/小毛 = fat vs dust-sized drops |
| 打新 / 打土狗 | dǎxīn / dǎ tǔgǒu | hit the new | ape launches on-chain | on-chain version of A-share IPO punting; tools: 防貔貅检测 honeypot checkers |
| 单币质押 | dānbì zhìyā | single-coin staking | single-sided staking | the "safe" option sold to leeks who fear 无常损失 |

## Proverbs & sayings (币圈语录)

| Saying | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 牛市不言顶，熊市不言底 | niúshì bù yán dǐng | "in a bull don't call the top, in a bear don't call the bottom" | the trend-respect proverb, quoted before every blow-off top |
| [[yi-bi-yi-bie-shu\|一币一别墅]] / 别墅靠大海 | yī bì yī biéshù | "one coin, one villa (by the sea)" | see atomic note; the canonical moonboy prayer |
| 一顿操作猛如虎，一看战绩两块五 | yī dùn cāozuò měng rú hǔ | "operated fierce as a tiger; account shows ¥2.50" | self-mockery for overtrading into dust; imported from soccer/stock memes |
| [[sha-bi-de-gong-shi\|傻逼的共识也是共识]] | shǎbī de gòngshí yě shì gòngshí | "the consensus of idiots is still consensus" | see atomic note; Li Xiaolai's leaked-tape realpolitik of value |
| 信仰不能当饭吃 | xìnyǎng bùnéng dāng fàn chī | "faith can't be eaten as rice" | rebuttal to 信仰-holders; conviction doesn't pay rent |
| 会买的是徒弟，会卖的是师傅 | — | "buying makes you an apprentice, selling makes you a master" | A-share wisdom; full version adds 会空仓的是祖师爷 "staying in cash makes you the grandmaster" |
| 利好出尽是利空 | lìhǎo chūjìn shì lìkōng | "good news exhausted becomes bad news" | sell-the-news, in four-character-idiom cadence |
| 你不理财，财不理你 | nǐ bù lǐcái, cái bù lǐ nǐ | "ignore your finances and they ignore you" | wealth-management ad slogan, quoted ironically after losses |
| 币圈没有新鲜事 | bìquān méiyǒu xīnxiānshì | "nothing new under the coin-circle sun" | every mania is a rerun; the 老韭菜's world-weary sigh |
| 拿住不动，别瞎折腾 | ná zhù bù dòng | "hold still, stop fiddling" | 囤币党 (hoarder-faction) HODL doctrine; 囤大饼 = stack BTC |
