# Coin Nicknames (币种绰号)

## Context
Nicknames for specific coins — mostly food puns and homophones, partly born as
censorship evasion after the 2017 mainland ban. See [[homophone-wordplay]] and
[[food-metaphors]] for the generating mechanics.

## Terms

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Coin | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[da-bing\|大饼]] | dàbǐng | big flatbread | BTC | see atomic note |
| 二饼 | èrbǐng | second flatbread | ETH | #2 after the big pancake |
| [[da-yi-tai\|姨太 / 大姨太]] | yítài | concubine | ETH (+forks) | see atomic note |
| 柚子 | yòuzi | pomelo | EOS | 谐音: sounds like "EOS" |
| 辣条 | làtiáo | spicy snack strips | LTC | 莱特 láitè sound-ish; cheap snack fits "budget BTC" |
| 大姨妈 (?) | dàyímā | "big aunt" (slang: period) | IOTA | MIOTA ≈ dàyímā sound meme (?) |
| 狗狗币 | gǒugoubì | doggy coin | DOGE | straight translation |
| 屎币 | shǐbì | shit coin | SHIB | perfect bilingual pun: SHIB ≈ shǐbì; embraced by its own community |
| 土狗 | tǔgǒu | dirt dog | any degen micro-cap | rural stray dog, no pedigree; antonym 金狗 "golden dog" = the shitcoin that 100x'd; 打狗 = sniping launches |
| 主流币 | zhǔliúbì | mainstream coin | majors/blue chips | |

| 泰达 / U | tàidá / "U" | — | USDT | everything is priced in "U"; 出U/收U = OTC sell/buy; U本位 = USDT-margined |
| 太子 | tàizǐ | crown prince | BCH | the fork positioned as heir to the BTC throne; extends the imperial-household frame of [[da-yi-tai]] |
| 澳本聪币 (?) | Àoběncōng bì | "Aussie-fake-Satoshi coin" | BSV | named for Craig Wright's nickname 澳本聪 (澳 Australia + 本聪 from 中本聪 Satoshi) — "the Australian who claims to be Satoshi"; the person-nickname is ubiquitous, the compound with 币 less attested |
| 艾达币 / 阿达 (?) | Àidábì / Ādá | — | ADA / Cardano | 艾达币 is the standard transliteration; 阿达 as affectionate short form unverified (?); Cardano officially 卡尔达诺; long dev cycles mocked as PPT链 "PowerPoint chain" (?) |
| 波卡 | Bōkǎ | wave card | DOT / Polkadot | official transliteration that reads like a nickname; ecosystem slots = 插槽拍卖 |

| 火腿 | huǒtuǐ | ham | HT (Huobi Token) | 火 from 火币 + food-pun pattern |
