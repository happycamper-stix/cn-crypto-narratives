# 傻逼的共识也是共识 (shǎbī de gòngshí yě shì gòngshí)

**Literal:** "the consensus of dumbasses is also consensus." **Actual:** value is
whatever enough believers agree it is — the coin circle's most cynical theory of
everything, quotable for any pump you don't respect.

## Origin
From the July 2018 "李笑来录音门" (Li Xiaolai tape-gate). Li Xiaolai — ex-New
Oriental English teacher, self-proclaimed largest BTC holder in China, and the
closest thing CN crypto has to a founding celebrity — was secretly recorded in a
profanity-laced private rant. The leaked hour torched half the industry by name,
dismissed various chains as worthless, and delivered the thesis: 价值就是共识，
傻逼的共识也是共识 — "value is consensus, and the consensus of idiots counts."

The tape was a scandal *and* a scripture. It said out loud what the trade already
priced: narratives beat fundamentals, and mocking a memecoin's holders doesn't stop
their consensus from making them rich. The line is now deployed both ways — as a
justification for buying 土狗, and as an epitaph when the consensus evaporates.
