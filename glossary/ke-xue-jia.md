# 科学家 (kēxuéjiā) — "The Scientists"

**Literal:** scientist. **Actual:** on-chain technical operators — bot writers,
MEV searchers, mint snipers, exploit arbitrageurs. The people who beat you to the
block.

## Origin
Coined during the 2017–2020 era (Fomo3D, early DeFi, NFT mints) when ordinary users
kept losing races to actors scripting contract calls directly. Calling them
"scientists" is half sneer, half awe: they read the contract, you read the Telegram.
When a gas war ends in 0.3 seconds, chat says 又被科学家抢了 — "the scientists
grabbed it again."

The term marks a class line inside the degen world: 韭菜 lose to 庄家 by
information, but lose to 科学家 by *technology*. A 撸毛工作室 running thousands of
Sybil wallets (see [[nv-wu]]) is scientists industrialized.

## Usage
- 防科学家机制 — anti-bot mechanics in a launch design (whitelists, commit-reveal)
- 科学家一键清池 — "scientists drained the pool in one click" (exploit reports)
