# 天台见 (tiāntái jiàn) — "See You on the Rooftop"

**Literal:** see you on the rooftop. **Actual:** the liquidation suicide joke —
"I'm so rekt I'm heading to the roof to jump." Said after 爆仓, almost never
meant, always understood.

## Origin
The meme predates crypto — and predates 2018: rooftop jokes from wiped-out
sports bettors (上天台 "going up to the rooftop", 天台已满 "the rooftop is
full") date to the 2014 Brazil World Cup, the June 2015 A-share crash turned
天台已满 into a stock-market staple (天台已满，股民跳楼需预约), and 天台见
itself is attested by 2015 before exploding mainstream during the 2018 FIFA
World Cup, when upset results wiped out bettors en masse. Crypto's contract
gamblers — the same demographic, on 125x instead of parlays — had adopted it
by the 2018–19 bear market, whose wave of real 爆仓 despair (rooftop threats
and protests outside exchange offices during 维权 campaigns; the June 2019
惠轶 suicide) gave the joke its permanent dark undertone. See the incident
table in [[he-yue-perps-slang]].

## The VIP-tier bit
A recurring elaboration turns the rooftop into a velvet-rope venue, scaled to
your losses — 天台VIP and rooftop-queue gags (天台排队, 不配上天台) are well
attested in both stock and crypto usage. One circulating version: "你才亏几万?
往下走几层排队 — 天台VIP位要亏20万以上，顶楼VVIP是亏百万的" ("You only lost
tens of thousands? Go queue a few floors down — rooftop VIP starts at 200k
losses; the penthouse VVIP is for the million-plus club") (?). Loss size as
status — the perps register's purest piece of gallows humor, structurally
identical to 关灯吃面 one-downmanship in [[trading-slang]].

## Usage
- 百倍开多，插针了，天台见 — "100x long, got wicked, see you on the roof"
- 天台又要排队了 — "the rooftop queue is forming again," said market-wide on
  cascade days (312, 519, 10·11)
- Often paired with 重开 ("restart [life]") and answered with 上岸吧
  ([[shang-an]]) by the merciful
