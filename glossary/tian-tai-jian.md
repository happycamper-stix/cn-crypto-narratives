# 天台见 (tiāntái jiàn) — "See You on the Rooftop"

**Literal:** see you on the rooftop. **Actual:** the liquidation suicide joke —
"I'm so rekt I'm heading to the roof to jump." Said after 爆仓, almost never
meant, always understood.

## Origin
The meme predates crypto: it exploded during the 2018 FIFA World Cup on Chinese
social media, where sports bettors wiped out by upset results posted 天台见 /
上天台 ("going up to the rooftop") and the stock joke 天台已满 ("the rooftop is
full, please queue"). Crypto's contract gamblers — the same demographic, on
125x instead of parlays — adopted it within months, and the 2018–19 wave of
real 爆仓 despair (rooftop threats and protests outside exchange offices during
维权 campaigns; the June 2019 惠轶 suicide) gave the joke its permanent dark
undertone. See the incident table in [[he-yue-perps-slang]].

## The VIP-tier bit
The canonical crypto elaboration turns the rooftop into a velvet-rope venue,
scaled to your losses: "你才亏几万? 往下走几层排队 — 天台VIP位要亏20万以上，
顶楼VVIP是亏百万的" ("You only lost tens of thousands? Go queue a few floors
down — rooftop VIP starts at 200k losses; the penthouse VVIP is for the
million-plus club"). Loss size as status — the perps register's purest piece of
gallows humor, structurally identical to 关灯吃面 one-downmanship in
[[trading-slang]].

## Usage
- 百倍开多，插针了，天台见 — "100x long, got wicked, see you on the roof"
- 天台又要排队了 — "the rooftop queue is forming again," said market-wide on
  cascade days (312, 519, 10·11)
- Often paired with 重开 ("restart [life]") and answered with 上岸吧
  ([[shang-an]]) by the merciful
