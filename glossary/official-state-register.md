# 官方话语: How Regulators Name Crypto (and How to Read It)

## Context
The vault decodes what the 币圈 says; this note decodes what the *state* says.
Every narrative shock the vault tracks — 94, 519/924, the HK pivot, the Feb 2026
upgrade — arrived as a document written in a distinct bureaucratic register
(官方话语 guānfāng huàyǔ, "official discourse") with its own nouns, verbs,
punctuation habits, and severity signals. Reading CN policy risk = parsing
exactly these words. The registers are mutually exclusive on purpose: which
word a speaker uses for "crypto" tells you whether they are a regulator, an
industry lobbyist, or a leek.

## The three-register partition

| Concept | Official / state media | Industry / HK-compliant | Community (币圈) |
|---|---|---|---|
| crypto generally | 虚拟货币 xūnǐ huòbì "virtual currency" (always pejorative, often 所谓 "so-called" + scare quotes) | 加密资产/数字资产 jiāmì/shùzì zīchǎn "crypto/digital assets"; HK SFC: 虚拟资产 xūnǐ zīchǎn | 币 bì, 大饼, tickers |
| trading | (交易)炒作 (jiāoyì) chǎozuò "trading hype/speculation" | 交易 "trading" | 炒币 chǎobì "stir-fry coins", 冲 |
| a token | 代币 dàibì "substitute-currency token" (echoes 代币票券, the banned merchant scrip of PBoC-law art. 20) | 通证 tōngzhèng "pass-token" (the 链圈's sanitized translation) | 币, 土狗 |
| Bitcoin's legal nature | 特定的虚拟商品 tèdìng de xūnǐ shāngpǐn "a specific virtual commodity" (2013, still the operative civil-law hook) | — | 数字黄金 "digital gold" |
| NFT | NFT金融化风险; sanctioned form renamed 数字藏品 shùzì cángpǐn "digital collectible" | 数字藏品 | NFT, 小图片 "little pictures" |
| CBDC | 数字人民币 shùzì rénmínbì / 法定数字货币 "legal digital currency" (e-CNY) | — | DCEP |

Key asymmetry: in the official register **数字货币 "digital currency" is
reserved for the state's own e-CNY**; private crypto never gets 货币 without
the delegitimizing 虚拟 prefix or quotes. Conversely the community's neutral
自称 (炒币, 币圈) appears in state media only inside condemnations
(币圈乱象 "coin-circle chaos"). When a People's Daily commentary borrowed the
community's own 空气币 "air coin" (Oct 2019), that was slang crossing *into*
officialese as an insult — the reverse never happens.

## The verb ladder (severity is a vocabulary choice)

| Verb | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning in documents | Landmark use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 防范 | fángfàn | guard against | baseline stance; in every title since 2013 | 《关于防范比特币风险的通知》2013 |
| 提示风险 | tíshì fēngxiǎn | point out risk | genre marker of a mere warning | 2018-8-24 风险提示 |
| 叫停 | jiàotíng | call a halt | state-media headline verb for immediate stop orders | "央行叫停ICO" Sept 2017 |
| 清理整顿 | qīnglǐ zhěngdùn | clean up & rectify | shut down a sector's domestic operations | 2017 exchange closures by the 整治办 |
| 处置 | chǔzhì | dispose / handle | escalation from 防范: not just warn, *deal with*; also 司法处置 of seized coins | added to the title in 2021: 防范和**处置** |
| 整治 | zhěngzhì | rectify (campaign-style) | a coordinated multi-agency campaign with quotas | 挖矿整治, 专项整治 |
| 清退 | qīngtuì | clear out & return | forced, compensated-in-kind expulsion of users/projects; the compliance verb exchanges echo | Huobi 2021-9-26: 有序清退 mainland 存量用户 |
| 有序退出/关停 | yǒuxù tuìchū | orderly exit/shutdown | wind-down without compensation; "orderly" = don't expect relief | 1283号文 on mining 存量项目 |
| 遏制 | èzhì | curb / contain | stop a trend from becoming financialized | 2022-4 NFT 倡议: 坚决遏制NFT金融化 |
| 取缔 | qǔdì | outlaw / ban | legal abolition; strongest administrative verb | 237号文: 一律严格禁止，坚决依法取缔 |
| 打击 | dǎjī | strike / crack down | police-register; when it appears, arrests follow | 金融委 2021-5-21: 打击比特币挖矿和交易行为 |

Decoder rule: a document's real content is its *verbs*. 防范+提示 = you may
carry on nervously; 处置/整治/清退 = the business model dies; 打击 = people
go to prison.

## The characterization ladder — one noun phrase per era

| Date | Document | Issuers | The load-bearing phrase |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2017-09-04 | 《关于防范代币发行融资风险的公告》("94公告") | 7 部委 | 代币发行融资 (dàibì fāxíng róngzī — the official name for ICO) 本质上是一种未经批准非法公开融资的行为，涉嫌非法发售代币票券、非法发行证券以及**非法集资**、金融诈骗、传销等**违法犯罪活动** |
| 2018-08-24 | 《关于防范以"虚拟货币""区块链"名义进行非法集资的风险提示》 | 5 部门 | 不法分子打着"金融创新""区块链"旗号…以"静态收益""动态收益"为诱饵 — the anti-fraud template ([[patriotic-coin-narratives]]) |
| 2021-05-18 | 三协会《关于防范虚拟货币交易炒作风险的公告》 | 互金/银行/支付清算协会 | 交易炒作 framing; the trial balloon that detonated 519 the next day |
| 2021-05-21 | 金融委第五十一次会议通稿 | State Council FSDC | **打击**比特币挖矿和交易行为，坚决防范个体风险向社会领域传递 — first State-Council-level 打击 |
| 2021-09-24 | 银发〔2021〕237号《…防范和处置虚拟货币**交易炒作**风险的通知》("924") | 10 部门 (incl. 最高法、最高检、公安部) | 虚拟货币相关业务活动属于**非法金融活动**; offshore exchanges serving 境内居民 included; 损失由参与当事人**自行承担** |
| 2021-09-24 | 发改运行〔2021〕1283号《关于整治虚拟货币"挖矿"活动的通知》 | 11 部门 | mining listed 淘汰类 (táotài lèi, "to-be-eliminated" industry class); 存量项目有序退出、严禁增量; 差别电价 punitive power pricing; 能耗双控 energy-quota accounting |
| 2022-04 | 三协会《关于防范NFT相关金融风险的倡议》 | 3 协会 | 坚决遏制NFT金融化证券化倾向 — birth of the de-coined 数字藏品 lane |
| 2024-08-29 | 上海徐汇《涉刑事诉讼虚拟币处置规范指引》 | district 检察院+公安 | 涉案虚拟货币 (shè'àn — "case-involved") = the judicial register for seized coins; disposal via offshore 变现 agents |
| 2026-02-06 | 银发〔2026〕42号《…防范和处置虚拟货币**等相关**风险的通知》 | 8 部门, **经国务院同意** | scope word 等 swallows 稳定币 + RWA: 未经…同意，境内外任何单位和个人不得在境外发行**挂钩人民币的稳定币**; RWA 原则禁止、经批准例外; company names may not contain "虚拟货币""虚拟资产""加密货币""加密资产""稳定币""现实世界资产代币化""RWA" |

The nouns escalate: 虚拟商品 (2013, a thing you may own) → 违法犯罪活动 (2017,
crimes) → 非法金融活动 (2021, a *category* ban on the business itself) →
虚拟货币**等** (2026, an open-ended class that eats each new industry coinage
— even the English letters "RWA" are now a regulated string in the company
registry). Courts kept the 2013 commodity hook, which is why criminal courts
protect stolen coins as property while civil courts void trading contracts as
违背公序良俗 (gōngxù liángsú, "public order and good morals") — losses
风险自担 (fēngxiǎn zìdān, "bear your own risk").

## Typographic and rhetorical tells

- **Scare quotes**: official texts write 虚拟货币"挖矿" — never bare 挖矿 —
  and 所谓"虚拟货币" (suǒwèi, "so-called"). The quotation marks are policy:
  a refusal to concede the activity's self-description. If a state text drops
  the quotes, an editor erred, not the line.
- **等 (děng, "etc.")**: the scope-expander. 2021 title: 交易炒作风险; 2026
  title: 虚拟货币**等**相关风险. One character = "and whatever you invent
  next." Analysts diff the titles.
- **经国务院同意 (jīng Guówùyuàn tóngyì, "with State Council approval")**:
  appeared for the first time in the 2026 notice — hierarchy signal that
  outranks raw department count.
- **不法分子 (bùfǎ fènzǐ, "lawless elements") + 打着…旗号/借…之名 ("under
  the banner of / in the name of")**: the fraud-bulletin frame. Whatever noun
  follows 打着 is officially a scam vector this season (区块链 2018, 元宇宙
  2022, 稳定币 2025 — Shenzhen's July 2025 alert on stablecoin-branded
  非法集资).
- **乱象 (luànxiàng, "chaotic phenomena")**: state-media word for the 币圈 as
  a genre of social disorder; a 乱象-survey article often precedes a 整治.
- **风险自担 / 自行承担**: the no-bailout clause — simultaneously a threat
  ("courts will not help you") and, read closely, an admission that holding
  itself is not prosecuted. The community reads this clause the way Kremlin-
  ologists read Pravda.

## The sanctioned divide: 链 good, 币 bad

The state's own binary maps exactly onto the community's 链圈/币圈 status war
([[suffix-morphology]]): 区块链 is a national strategic technology; 币 is the
contraband part. Canon:

- **2019-10-24**: Xi, Politburo 18th collective study session — 把区块链作为
  核心技术自主创新重要突破口 ("blockchain as a key breakthrough for
  indigenous core-technology innovation"). Every 国产 ticker pumped;
  the speech is the community's proof-text that "the state loves our bags."
- **2019-10-29**: People's Daily commentary corrects the trade: 区块链技术
  创新**不等于**炒作虚拟货币 ("blockchain innovation ≠ hyping virtual
  currency") — 防止利用区块链发行虚拟货币、炒作空气币.
- **[[wu-bi-qu-kuai-lian|无币区块链]]** (wúbì qūkuàiliàn, "coinless
  blockchain") — the only permitted on-chain lane: consortium chains (联盟链),
  BSN, court/e-invoice chains. Community rejoinder: 太监链 ("eunuch chain") (?).
- **数字藏品** — NFTs admitted into the sanctioned lane by amputating the
  market: RMB pricing, real-name, no secondary trading, 转赠 (gifting) only.
  The renaming *is* the regulation.

Heuristic: any mainland-legible pitch must be phrased as 链 not 币 — which is
why scam decks say 区块链项目 and 通证 while their WeChat groups say 币.

## Reading a policy document like an analyst

1. **Count the departments, then read the roster.** 5 (2013) → 7 (2017) → 10
   + 11 (2021) → 8 + 经国务院同意 (2026). More agencies = wider campaign; but
   *which* agencies matters more: 公安部 present → arrests; 最高法/最高检
   (2021 only) → criminal-judicial follow-through; 网信办 → account and
   media purges; 外汇局 → capital-flight framing; 发改委 → it's about
   electricity ([[cn-crypto-eras-timeline]]).
2. **Genre ladder**: 风险提示 (warning) < 倡议 (industry "initiative",
   association-issued) < 公告 (declaratory announcement) < 通知 (operative,
   numbered 银发〔year〕N号, binds implementation). Association statements
   (三协会) are the trial balloon; the ministry 通知 is the hammer —
   May 18 → Sept 24, 2021 ran exactly this sequence.
3. **Diff the title against the last cycle's.** 防范 → 防范和处置;
   交易炒作风险 → 等相关风险. Two characters of drift = a new prohibited
   industry.
4. **Read the verbs** (ladder above) and the **答记者问** (dá jìzhě wèn,
   the accompanying staged "reporter Q&A") — scope details, carve-outs, and
   enforcement priorities live there, not in the notice body.
5. **Watch what is re-affirmed as good.** Every crackdown text carries the
   无币 carve-out sentence ("support blockchain technology innovation"). Its
   absence would be the true bear signal; its presence fuels the perennial
   "the state is secretly pro-crypto" hopium that HK news launders for
   mainland KOLs ([[hk-tw-mainland-divergence]]).
6. **Then remember the market reflex**: every entry in the ladder above was a
   sell-the-news crash later reframed as a buy — 靴子落地 (xuēzi luòdì, "the
   boot has dropped") is the community's relief idiom once the document is
   finally published ([[number-slang]]: 94, 519, 924).

## Enforcement register (police & courts)

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 非法集资 | fēifǎ jízī | illegal fund-gathering | illegal fundraising — the workhorse charge since 94; umbrella for 非法吸收公众存款 & 集资诈骗 |
| 非法经营罪 | fēifǎ jīngyíng zuì | illegal business operation crime | catch-all for running exchanges/OTC desks post-924 |
| 传销 | chuánxiāo | pass-along selling | pyramid/MLM recruiting — the charge for 模式币/资金盘 schemes |
| 帮信罪 | bāngxìn zuì | "assisting-information" crime | 帮助信息网络犯罪活动罪; what OTC sellers and card-lenders get charged with — the legal engine behind [[dong-ka]] |
| 断卡行动 | duànkǎ xíngdòng | "card-breaking" operation | Oct 2020– campaign freezing mule bank cards/SIMs; why every OTC trade risks a frozen card |
| 涉案虚拟货币 | shè'àn xūnǐ huòbì | case-involved virtual currency | seized/confiscated coins awaiting 司法处置 (judicial disposal), usually liquidated offshore via licensed agents — a growing, quietly pragmatic register |
| 专项整治 | zhuānxiàng zhěngzhì | special rectification | campaign-mode governance; the 互联网金融风险专项整治 (2016–20) office (整治办) executed the 94 clean-up |
