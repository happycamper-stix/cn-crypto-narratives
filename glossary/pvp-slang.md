# PvP & Rotation-Battle Slang (对打黑话)

## Context
Vocabulary for coins fighting each other over the same rotation — the zero-sum
register of the meme casino. English "PvP" was adopted directly into Chinese
crypto usage from gaming and now anchors a small native cluster. Grammar of the
battlefield itself is in [[bankuai-longtou-rotation-slang]].

## Terms

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PVP / PVP盘 | — | player-vs-player | zero-sum meme trading — no outside money, traders harvesting each other | direct gaming loan; opposite of PVE (fresh retail inflow to farm) |
| 对打 | duìdǎ | hit each other | two coins/camps actively fighting for the same attention/liquidity | the head-to-head framing itself |
| 内战 | nèizhàn | civil war | rivalry inside one 板块 (two dogs of the same meta) | e.g. two CZ-adjacency coins splitting the faithful |
| 吸血 | xīxuè | suck blood | one asset's pump draining liquidity from everything else | classically BTC 吸血 alts; between memes it means the winner vampires the loser |
| 虹吸 / 虹吸效应 | hóngxī | siphon (effect) | the siphon dynamic behind 吸血 — attention/liquidity flows to the leader | more analytical register than 吸血 |
| 轮动 | lúndòng | rotate | rotation — money moving sector to sector, coin to coin | the master dynamic PvP battles decide; see [[bankuai-longtou-rotation-slang]] |
| 接力 | jiēlì | relay race | the next coin picking up a finished runner's crowd | "who gets the baton" — losers of a PvP often 接力 late |
| 分流 | fēnliú | split the flow | attention/capital splitting between two candidates, weakening both | a PvP with no winner: both bleed |
| 龙一 / 龙二 | lóng yī / lóng èr | dragon #1 / #2 | the ranked leaders of a hot 板块 | PvP is often the fight over who IS 龙一; 龙二 dies first in a downturn |
| 卡位 | qiǎwèi | seize the slot | rushing to occupy a narrative slot before a rival | launchpad-era: first credible token of a fresh meta wins the slot |

## Decoding heuristic
When two coins share a meta, ask: which is 龙一 (leader), is the flow 吸血 (one
winning) or 分流 (both bleeding), and what event would 轮动 the money — a 一姐
reply, a listing, or leader exhaustion. See [[binance-gravity-well]] and
[[launchpad-naming-meta]] for the usual flip triggers.
