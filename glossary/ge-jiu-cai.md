# 割韭菜 (gē jiǔcài) — "Cutting Leeks"

**Literal:** cut leeks. **Actual:** dumping on retail; fleecing the crowd. To 被割
("be cut") is to be exit liquidity.

## Mechanics
The harvesting verb for [[jiu-cai]]: insiders/whales/teams (项目方, 庄家) pump,
distribute (出货), and cut. Because leeks regrow, the cut is expected to repeat —
the term encodes cynicism about the *cycle*, not a one-off scam.

## Usage
- 项目方又割了一波韭菜 — "the team harvested another round of leeks"
- 割一波就走 — "cut one wave and leave": the fast extractive soft-rug launch mentality

## English mapping
Closest: "dumping on retail" / "rugging", but 割韭菜 is broader — any structural
wealth transfer from retail to insiders, including legal ones.
