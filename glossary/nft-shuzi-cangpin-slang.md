# NFT-Era Slang & the 数字藏品 Compliance Register (2021–22)

## Context
The 2021–22 NFT boom produced the sharpest register split in the whole CN crypto
lexicon. One technology, two vocabularies: offshore Chinese-speaking traders
(on Ethereum/Solana, via VPN, priced in ETH) spoke a calqued OpenSea dialect —
**小图片, 地板价, 白名单, 铸造** — while mainland platforms, forced away from
anything smelling of 代币 (dàibì, "token"), rebuilt the entire product as
**数字藏品** (shùzì cángpǐn, "digital collectibles"): no wallet, no gas, no
secondary market, real-name accounts, RMB pricing, consortium chains.

**The one-word tell**: whether a speaker says *NFT* or *数藏* (shùcáng) locates
them instantly on one side of the compliance perimeter. Say NFT/小图片/地板价
and you're offshore, wallet-native, ETH-denominated. Say 数藏/藏品/优先购 and
you're inside the wall, on 鲸探 or a 野鸡平台, settled in RMB under your real
name. Offshore CT uses 数藏 only in scare quotes or as a punchline; mainland
platforms scrubbed the very letters "NFT" from their apps. This is
[[censorship-evasion-coinage]] operating at the *product* level rather than the
word level — instead of camouflaging the word, the industry re-engineered the
thing so the word no longer applied.

## Timeline of the compliance perimeter
- **2021-06-23** — Alipay × 敦煌美术研究所 release 敦煌飞天/九色鹿 payment-code
  skins (16,000 copies, ¥9.9 + 10 Alipay points) on 蚂蚁链粉丝粒 (Mǎyǐliàn
  fěnsīlì, "AntChain fan grain") — the mainland's proto-NFT moment. Within a day
  listings on 闲鱼 (Xiányú, Alibaba's flea-market app) hit **¥1.5M**; on June 24
  Xianyu nuked the keyword "NFT" entirely.
- **2021-08** — Tencent launches **幻核** (Huànhé, "Phantom Core"), debut drop of
  audio collectibles from talk show 《十三邀》.
- **2021-10-14** — 《非同质化权益（NFR）白皮书》 published (China Mobile
  Communications Association et al., ed. 蔡维德): proposes **NFR** 非同质化权益
  (fēi tóngzhìhuà quányì, "non-fungible *rights*") as a token-free, law-first
  Chinese替代 for NFT. The acronym surgery — swap Token for Rights — is the
  compliance register in miniature. (NFR never caught on commercially.)
- **2021-10-23** — Ant and Tencent simultaneously scrub every instance of "NFT"
  from their apps, replacing it with **数字藏品**. Ant defines holdings as
  虚拟数字商品 ("virtual digital goods"), Tencent as 虚拟权益证明 ("virtual
  rights proofs") — two different legal hedges for the same JPEG.
- **2021-12** — 粉丝粒 rebrands as **鲸探** (Jīngtàn, "Whale Explore"); Xinhua
  News Agency issues free "news digital collectibles" (Dec 24) — state media
  blessing the compliant form while state policy bans the offshore one.
- **2022-04-13** — 中国互联网金融协会 + 中国银行业协会 + 中国证券业协会 issue
  《关于防范NFT相关金融风险的倡议》: no fractionalization ("变相ICO"), no
  centralized/continuous listing or standardized-contract trading, no pricing
  in BTC/ETH/USDT, real-name only, RMB settlement only. The document renders
  NFT as **非同质化通证** (fēi tóngzhìhuà tōngzhèng) — pointedly *not*
  非同质化**代币**: 通证 "circulating certificate" vs 代币 "substitute
  currency," a translation fight running since the 2018 通证经济 movement
  (孟岩's coinage). Self-regulatory 倡议, not law — the era ran in a 监管真空
  (regulatory vacuum) the whole time.
- **2022-05-17** — **iBox** crash: flagship collections −70–80% in a day; the
  数藏 speculative top is in.
- **2022-08-16** — Tencent's 幻核 halts issuance and offers refunds — the first
  big-tech full retreat; industry reads it as the official obituary.
- **2022-11** — 20+ platforms post 清退公告 (wind-down notices), refunding
  5–30% of purchase price.
- **2023-01-01** — 中国数字资产交易平台 ("China Digital Asset Trading
  Platform," short-styled 中数交/CDEX) launches in Beijing — a state-built attempt at a
  "compliant secondary market" for digital collectibles/copyright. Little
  traction.
- **2023** — 鲸探 quietly shortens 转赠 lockups: 180d→90d (Feb), then →24h
  (Aug). OTC prices *fell* on the Feb news — the grey market had been pricing
  the scarcity created by the lockup itself.

## Register 1 — offshore NFT slang (the OpenSea dialect)

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 小图片 | xiǎo túpiàn | little picture | NFTs, esp. PFPs | the self-deprecating genre name ("买小图片" = buying JPEGs); exact CN twin of "jpegs"; post-crash usage turns elegiac: "小图片故事已完结" |
| 地板价 | dìbǎnjià | floor price | lowest listed price | direct calque; the era's health metric; 地板 alone = "the floor" |
| 扫地板 | sǎo dìbǎn | sweep the floor | bulk-buy the cheapest listings | calque of "floor sweep"; whale bullish signal |
| 白名单 | báimíngdān | white list | whitelist/WL — pre-sale mint right | the era's status currency; 白单/大白 variants; multi-account WL farming is what made 女巫 (sybil) checks necessary — see [[nv-wu]] |
| 肝白 | gān bái | liver the white | grind for whitelist | 肝 "liver" = gaming slang for unhealthy all-night grinding; 肝Discord等级, fan-art contests, 邀请拉人; media quip: "NFT有多火，白名单就有多卷" (卷 = involuted) |

| 红豆 | hóngdòu | red bean | Azuki | azuki *is* Japanese for red bean; the CN community adopted the translation as the pet name |
| 头像项目 | tóuxiàng xiàngmù | avatar project | PFP collection | 换头像 (changing your avatar to the ape) = the public act of joining |

| 白嫖 | báipiáo | white-prostitute (= freeload) | freemint culture | summer-2022 goblintown-style free mints; 白嫖党 = the freeload faction; reused inside 数藏圈 for airdrop hunters |
| 赋能 | fùnéng | empower | "utility" | corporate buzzword repurposed; 空气赋能 = vaporware utility; shared by both registers — the one word 数藏 platforms and NFT projects used identically |

| NFT土狗 | — tǔgǒu | dirt-dog NFT | low-effort derivative mint | 土狗 carried over from memecoins; hyped mint → 破发 → 归零 pipeline |
| 右键保存 | yòujiàn bǎocún | right-click save | right-clicker mockery | the anti-NFT taunt, same joke worldwide: "我右键保存了你的猴子" |

Related era species: **STEPN** (跑鞋, pǎoxié, "running shoes") — the
run-to-earn app with a massive CN user base that announced in May 2022 it would
**清退** (qīngtuì, "clear out") mainland users by July 15, 2022, adopting the
regulator's own bureaucratic verb for expelling its own customers. GameFi's
打金 (dǎjīn, "hitting gold" — MMO gold-farming slang) also dates from this era.

## Register 2 — the official compliance register

The design principle: delete every load-bearing crypto noun and substitute a
cultural-heritage or consumer-rights noun. What remains is a blockchain product
described entirely in the language of museum gift shops.

| Compliance term | Pinyin | Literal | Replaces / actual function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 数字藏品 | shùzì cángpǐn | digital collectible | "NFT"; 藏品 is museum/collector vocabulary — frames the buyer as 收藏家 not investor; abbreviation 数藏 |
| 非同质化通证 | fēi tóngzhìhuà tōngzhèng | non-fungible certificate | official rendering of NFT (April 2022 倡议), dodging 代币 "token" |
| 非同质化权益 (NFR) | fēi tóngzhìhuà quányì | non-fungible rights | the Oct 2021 white-paper attempt to replace the acronym itself |
| 联盟链 | liánméngliàn | alliance chain | consortium chain replacing 公链 (public chain): 蚂蚁链 (Ant), 至信链 (Zhìxìnliàn, Tencent, built on 长安链), 文昌链 (Wénchāngliàn, BSN) — permissioned, censorable, no token |
| 无币区块链 | wúbì qūkuàiliàn | coinless blockchain | the whole official framework: blockchain minus the coin, championed by BSN — see [[wu-bi-qu-kuai-lian]] (the 币圈 rejoinder: 太监链, "eunuch chain") |
| 发行 / 首发 | fāxíng / shǒufā | issue / first issue | replaces 铸造/mint; 发行方 = issuer (publisher register, not foundry register) |
| 优先购 | yōuxiāngòu | priority purchase | the compliant whitelist: hold collection X → buy the next drop minutes early; guides literally gloss it as 白名单 |
| 转赠 | zhuǎnzèng | transfer-gift | the sanctioned exit: "gifting" to a friend after a lockup (鲸探: 180 days, recipient 720 days) — the load-bearing euphemism the entire grey market ran through |
| 寄售 | jìshòu | consignment | how wildcat platforms said "secondary market" — 寄售市场 with platform fees of 4.5% (?) to 10% |
| 合成 | héchéng | synthesize | burn N commons → 1 rare; supply sink + engagement loop, straight from gacha games |
| 盲盒 / 隐藏款 | mánghé / yǐncángkuǎn | blind box / hidden edition | randomized drops; vocabulary imported wholesale from Popmart 潮玩 culture, giving 数藏 a ready-made native gambling idiom that needed no crypto words at all |
| 空投 | kōngtóu | airdrop | the one crypto mechanic kept *with its name*: free drops as compliant marketing (no purchase = no 炒作 accusation) |
| 确权 | quèquán | confirm rights | copyright-registration framing of what the chain is "for" |
| 实名 / 人民币结算 | shímíng / rénmínbì jiésuàn | real-name / RMB settlement | the perimeter conditions: no pseudonymous wallets, no ETH/USDT pricing |
| 理性收藏，拒绝炒作 | lǐxìng shōucáng, jùjué chǎozuò | collect rationally, refuse speculation | the boilerplate risk-warning liturgy on every drop page (exact stock wording varies: 理性消费/抵制炒作) (?) |
| 清退 | qīngtuì | clear out and return | orderly wind-down with partial refunds — the era's exit word (platforms 清退 users; STEPN 清退 the mainland) |

## Register 3 — 数藏圈 street slang (inside the wall)

The grey market's own dialect: crypto trading slang smuggled in, plus native
coinages for platform-specific mechanics.

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 数藏圈 / 藏友 | shùcángquān / cángyǒu | collectibles circle / collector-friend | the scene / its members | 藏友 borrowed from antiques forums — self-image as gentleman collectors, deployed while flipping JPEGs of museum artifacts |
| 开二级 | kāi èrjí | open the secondary | platform launches (or is *rumored* to launch) resale | the master catalyst: 开二级传闻 pumped primary drops the way exchange-listing rumors pump coins; per Aug 2022 reporting ~301 of ~998 platforms had opened one despite the 倡议 |
| 场外 | chǎngwài | off-venue | OTC resale in WeChat/QQ groups | screenshots + 转账 + trust; where 转赠 assets actually cleared; 场外价 = the real price of a "non-tradable" collectible |
| 科技 | kējì | technology | bots/scripts for grabbing drops | the 数藏 twin of 科学家; 上科技 = deploy bots; insiders describe launches "被科技撸走" (stripped by bots) |
| 拉盘 / 砸盘 / 洗售 | lāpán / zápán / xǐshòu | pull / smash the plate / wash-sell | pump / dump / wash-trade | imported unchanged from [[trading-slang]]; platforms themselves were the 庄家, seeding 老鼠仓 accounts and freezing sell orders |
| 老鼠仓 | lǎoshǔcāng | rat warehouse | insider pre-loaded accounts | platform staff holding inventory across sock-puppet accounts before a 拉盘 |
| 首发秒空 | shǒufā miǎokōng | first issue, emptied in seconds | instant sellout | the marketing metric; also 秒罄; manufactured via small supply + 老鼠仓 |
| 野鸡平台 | yějī píngtái | pheasant platform | fly-by-night 数藏 platform | vs the 大厂 (big-tech) trio 鲸探/幻核/灵稀 (JD); the 野鸡s are precisely the ones that 开二级 — and then 跑路 |
| 数藏维权 | shùcáng wéiquán | rights-defense | victim/refund groups | the era's terminal stage; QQ 维权群 in the hundreds per platform (?); iBox fraud complaints reached police case files in 2023 (?) |
| 00后的第一次理财 | línglínghòu de dìyīcì lǐcái | the post-00 generation's first investment | media tag for the student-heavy retail base | 数藏 was many mainland Gen-Z users' first speculative market — leek genealogy: their parents had A-shares, they got JPEGs ([[jiu-cai]]) |

## The tell, systematized

| Concept | Offshore register | Mainland register | What the word choice signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| the asset | NFT / 小图片 | 数字藏品 / 数藏 | which side of the perimeter you trade on |
| acquiring | mint / 铸造 / 打 | 首发 / 抢购 / 购藏 | foundry vs publishing metaphor |
| early access | 白名单 / WL | 优先购 | Discord grind vs platform loyalty perk |
| resale | 挂单 OpenSea / 二级市场 | 转赠 / 寄售 / 场外 | open market vs euphemized loophole |
| the rails | 公链 (以太坊) | 联盟链 (至信链/文昌链) | permissionless vs permissioned |
| unit of account | ETH / U | 人民币 | pseudonymous wallet vs 实名 bank card |
| the people | 玩家 / degen / 韭菜 | 藏友 / 用户 / 消费者 | casino vs gift-shop self-image |
| catastrophe | rug / 跑路 / 归零 | 清退 / 暴雷 / 维权 | anarchic exit vs administrative wind-down |
| the bots | 科学家 | 科技 | same practice, different noun |

Mixing registers is itself meaningful: a mainland platform that kept "NFT" in
its marketing after Oct 2021 was advertising regulatory recklessness (a 野鸡
tell); an offshore CT account saying 数藏 unironically is likely a mainland
newcomer; lawyers and academics writing 非同质化通证 are performing distance
from 币圈. Compare the parallel accent-tells in [[hk-tw-mainland-divergence]]
(HK regulator says 虚拟资产, mainland officialdom says 虚拟货币), and the wider
official/industry/community three-way partition in [[official-state-register]] —
this note is that partition caught mid-formation, in one product vertical.

## Dated set pieces (the era's shared references)
- **周同学幻想熊 & the stolen ape**: Jay Chou's PhantaBear (幻想熊, Ezek, Jan 1,
  2022, 10,000 units, ~US$10M in ~40 min) made NFTs a sinophone-pop event;
  on **April 1, 2022** his BAYC #3738 (a gift from 麻吉大哥) was phished —
  "不是愚人节玩笑" — and flipped on LooksRare at 111→130→155 ETH (~¥3.2M).
  The two moments bracket the mania: idol-mint euphoria to phishing-loss
  schadenfreude ("快发专辑止损" — "drop an album to stop the loss").
- **中国李宁无聊猿** (Apr 2022): Li Ning bought BAYC #4102 and fronted a
  streetwear pop-up with it — the peak of mainland brands touching *offshore*
  NFTs, weeks before the market broke.
- **胖虎打疫苗案** (Apr 20, 2022): Hangzhou Internet Court's "first NFT case" —
  platform NFTCN (原与宙) liable ¥4,000 for a user's mint of the 我不是胖虎
  fat-tiger cartoon; established that platforms owe more than notice-and-delete
  review. A separate late-2022 Hangzhou ruling treated 数字藏品 as protected
  网络虚拟财产 (network virtual property) (?).
- **幻核's obituary** (Aug 16, 2022): "腾讯成首个退出的互联网大厂" — when the
  most compliant player quit, the compliant category itself was read as dead.

## Analytic takeaways
1. **Register as compliance forensics**: 2021–22 minted the cleanest natural
   experiment in the vault — one referent, two vocabularies, chosen by legal
   position rather than taste. Every later era inherits the trick (HK's
   虚拟资产 register; 铭文-era platforms carefully never saying 代币).
2. **The euphemism did real work**: 转赠 wasn't decoration — the entire grey
   market was plumbed through the gifting function, and when 鲸探 shortened the
   lockup the *OTC price fell*, proving the euphemism had become market
   infrastructure.
3. **数藏 didn't borrow crypto's gambling idiom — it used Popmart's**: 盲盒,
   隐藏款, 合成 gave mainland platforms a fully native casino vocabulary with
   zero crypto fingerprints; the censor-proofing was inherited free of charge.
4. **The offshore register is calqued from English, not A-shares**: 钻石手,
   扫地板, 地板价 translate OpenSea-speak directly — unlike the 2017-era
   lexicon which translated stock-market Chinese into crypto. Direction of
   calque flow dates a term's era.
5. **Both registers share one word — 赋能** — and it meant the same nothing in
   each.
