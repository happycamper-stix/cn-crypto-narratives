# 二池 (èrchí) — "The Second Pool"

**Literal:** pool number two. **Actual:** in DeFi Summer farm design, the LP pool
pairing the farm's own token — highest APR, and where the massacre happens.

## Mechanics
2020-era food farms had a standard architecture the CN community numbered:
- 一池 (first pool): stake safe assets (USDT, ETH) to earn the farm token — low
  risk, you can't lose principal.
- 二池 (second pool): provide liquidity for FARM/USDT itself — triple-digit APR,
  but you're now holding the emission token both as LP and reward.

When emissions outrun demand, the token dumps; 二池 LPs eat the fall twice, through
无常损失 (impermanent loss) and through their farmed rewards going to zero. Hence
the proverb-shaped warning 一池保本，二池埋人 — "pool one preserves principal, pool
two buries people." 冲头矿 (rushing day-one emissions, then fleeing before 二池
collapses) became its own speed-run discipline, dominated by the 科学家 class
(see [[ke-xue-jia]]).

The numbering survives as a general risk shorthand: calling anything 二池 means
"the leg of this trade where you're the exit liquidity."
