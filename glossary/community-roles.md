# Community Roles & Identities (币圈众生)

## Context
Who's who in 币圈 (bìquān, "the coin circle"). The cast of the harvest metaphor:
leeks, farmers, whales, wool-pluckers. Note the parallel circles: 链圈 (blockchain
circle, the "serious" tech side) and 矿圈 (mining circle).

## Terms

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[jiu-cai\|韭菜]] | jiǔcài | leek | retail bagholder | see atomic note; 老韭菜 = scarred veteran (badge of honor), 新韭菜/小白 = newbie |
| 散户 | sǎnhù | scattered households | retail | standard stock term |
| 巨鲸 | jùjīng | giant whale | whale | calque of English |

| 庄家 / 狗庄 | zhuāngjiā / gǒuzhuāng | casino banker / dog banker | manipulator controlling a coin | from stock 坐庄; 狗 adds insult |
| 羊毛党 / 撸毛 | yángmáodǎng / lūmáo | wool party / pluck wool | airdrop farmers / farming | from bank-promo arbitrage 薅羊毛; 撸毛工作室 = Sybil farms with thousands of wallets |
| 打狗 | dǎgǒu | hit dogs | sniping 土狗 launches | degen occupation |
| V神 | V-shén | V God | Vitalik | deification suffix 神, coined early by CN community |

| 一姐 | yījiě | First Sister | He Yi (Binance) | pun on her name 一; CZ counterpart |
| 麻吉大哥 | Májí dàgē | Machi Big Brother | Jason Huang (黄立成) | 麻吉 = Taiwanese "buddy" (from "match"); famous public 归零 episodes |

| [[dai-dan-lao-shi\|带单老师]] | dàidān lǎoshī | order-leading teacher | scam trading guru | see atomic note; operates 喊单群 signal groups |
| 割总 | Gēzǒng | "President Cut" | any serial leek-cutter boss | 总 (CEO honorific) + 割; same flip as 孙割 — respect grammar weaponized into accusation |
| [[ke-xue-jia\|科学家]] | kēxuéjiā | scientist | on-chain bot/MEV/exploit operator | see atomic note; half insult, half awe |

| U商 / 承兑商 | U-shāng / chéngduìshāng | U-merchant / acceptance dealer | OTC desks buying/selling USDT for CNY | the grey off-ramp layer; source of [[dong-ka]] risk |
| 卡农 | kǎnóng | card farmer | person renting out bank cards to launder OTC flows | 农 as in farmer; the human infrastructure of dirty 出金 |
| 矿工 / 矿场 / 矿难 | kuànggōng / kuàngchǎng / kuàngnàn | miner / mine / mining disaster | miners / farms / GPU-price crash | 矿难 borrows the word for a real mine collapse; 矿圈 was its own social world pre-2021 ban |
| 车头 / 跟单 | chētóu / gēndān | locomotive / follow orders | trade leader / copy-trader | extends the 上车 bus metaphor: the 车头 drives, passengers follow; 翻车 "vehicle flips" = the call blows up |
| 澳本聪 | Àoběncōng | "Aussie Satoshi" | Craig Wright | 澳大利亚 + 中本聪 compressed into a three-character smirk: the title itself calls him fake |
| 木头姐 | Mùtou jiě | Sister Wood | Cathie Wood (ARK) | 木头 = literal "wood"; adopted as honorary crypto bull |
| 白嫖党 | báipiáodǎng | freeloader party | zero-cost farmers | 白嫖 (gaming slang: enjoy without paying) applied to grinding airdrops with no capital |
| 盘圈 / CX | pánquān / CX | scheme circle | the ponzi-scheme subculture | CX = pinyin initials of 传销 (MLM), used to dodge keyword censors; 盘圈人 migrate scheme to scheme |
