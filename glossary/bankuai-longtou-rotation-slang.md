# 板块/龙头: The A-Share Sector-Play Grammar of Chinese Crypto

## Context
Chinese crypto discourse doesn't organize the market by "narratives" the way
English CT does — it organizes it by **板块** (sectors), each with a **龙头**
(dragon-head leader), traded through **轮动** (rotation). This entire grammar is
imported wholesale from A-share 短线 (short-line/momentum) trading culture,
where a generation of retail traders learned 龙头战法 ("dragon-head tactics")
chasing limit-up boards. When they moved to crypto they brought the taxonomy
with them — which is why CN market recaps read like A-share 复盘 (fùpán,
post-session review) posts, and why [[meme-season-aftermath]] can call 我踏马来了
a 新龙头 and every reader knows exactly what phase of the cycle that implies.
This is the core mechanism by which Chinese narratives become trades: name the
板块 → crown the 龙头 → rotate down the hierarchy → 退潮.

A-share structural note: mainland main-board stocks have a ±10% daily limit (涨停板) and
T+1 settlement. The whole 打板/连板 vocabulary grew around that constraint. Crypto
has neither — so the *words* migrated but the mechanics compressed: what takes
an A-share theme two weeks of limit-up ladders takes a BSC meme sector 48 hours
(the 火山喷发式 "volcanic eruption" profile in [[meme-season-aftermath]]).

## The taxonomy: how the market is carved up

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 板块 | bǎnkuài | plate/slab | sector, thematic block | geology metaphor (tectonic plates); the atomic unit of CN market analysis — AI板块, 铭文板块, 动物币板块 |
| 赛道 | sàidào | race track | vertical, category | VC-register synonym of 板块: DeFi赛道, AI Agent赛道; a 板块 is traded, a 赛道 is "invested in" |
| 概念 / 概念币 | gàiniàn / gàiniànbì | concept / concept coin | coin whose value is an *association*, not a product | direct calque of A-share 概念股 (concept stocks); 香港概念, 马斯克概念, DeepSeek概念 — the association IS the thesis |
| 题材 | tícái | subject matter | tradeable theme | slightly more cynical than 概念: a 题材 exists to be 炒 (stir-fried/speculated) |
| 叙事 | xùshì | narration | narrative | the imported English "narrative"; formal register of 题材; media write 叙事, traders say 题材/概念 |
| 热点 | rèdiǎn | hot spot | the currently trending topic | what everything rotates toward; 追热点 = chase the heat |
| 风口 | fēngkǒu | wind mouth/vent | where the wind (capital flow) is blowing | from Lei Jun's startup proverb 站在风口上，猪都能飞 "on the wind vent, even a pig can fly" — apply to any 板块 receiving inflows |
| 财富密码 | cáifù mìmǎ | wealth password | the money-making formula of the moment | "任何一次板块轮动都是币圈的财富密码" — every rotation is a wealth code (AICoin) (?) |
| 妖币 | yāobì | demon coin | coin moving irrationally, detached from the market | from A-share 妖股 "monster stock": tiny float, banker-controlled (庄家), pure sentiment object; more dangerous than 空气币 — "空气币至少还画饼，妖币连饼都不画" (?) |

## The dragon hierarchy: who leads

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 龙头 | lóngtóu | dragon head | sector leader | also the ordinary word for "faucet" — the tap capital flows from; the coin that moves first, hardest, and defines the 板块's ceiling |
| 龙一 / 龙二 / 龙三 | lóng yī / èr / sān | dragon #1/#2/#3 | ranked leader hierarchy within a sector | live usage in meme trenches: Mar 2025 BSC 中东 meta had $Mubarak as 龙一, $Mansa as 龙二 |
| 新龙头 | xīn lóngtóu | new dragon head | leader of the *next* wave | crowning a 新龙头 = declaring a fresh sub-cycle; see 我踏马来了 in [[meme-season-aftermath]] |
| 补涨龙 | bǔzhǎnglóng | catch-up dragon | strongest follower once the leader tops out | funds "rebuild" the trade in a related name after the 龙头 has marked out the sector's space |
| 卡位 | kǎwèi | jam the slot | claiming leadership when the 龙头 falters | A-share tactic: a follower spikes against the trend the day the leader breaks, trying to *become* the new leader; used metaphorically in crypto |
| 跟风 / 跟风盘 | gēnfēng | follow the wind | follower coins / copycat money | the undifferentiated middle of a 板块; 跟风股识别 is its own A-share sub-literature |
| 杂毛 | zámáo | stray fur, mongrel | junk names in the theme, non-leaders | pejorative; the discipline slogan: 只干龙头，不干杂毛 "only trade the dragon-head, never the mutts" |
| 龙头效应 / 板块效应 | lóngtóu xiàoyìng | dragon-head / sector effect | leader's move lifts the whole sector | the mechanism 补涨 depends on: leader proves the theme, capital fans out |

The dinner-table hierarchy proverb: 龙头吃肉，跟风喝汤 — "the dragon-head eats
meat, the followers drink soup" (and late entries 连汤都喝不上, don't even get
soup). Extends the 吃肉/吃面 outcome idiom in [[trading-slang]].

## Rotation mechanics: the verbs

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 轮动 / 板块轮动 | lúndòng | wheel-turn / sector rotation | capital cycling from sector to sector | THE master concept; CN analysts map every bull leg as a rotation schedule (each 板块 gets ~2–3 weeks in classic altseason models, hours in meme season) |
| 补涨 | bǔzhǎng | patch-rise | catch-up pump | laggards rising after the leader has already run; described as 踏空资金慌乱的选择 — "the panicked choice of capital that missed the move" (?) |
| 高低切 | gāodīqiē | high-low cut | rotating out of extended names into laggards | short for 高低位切换 (high/low-position switch); triggered when high-位 leaders start造成亏钱效应; on CN CT applied to e.g. majors→laggard alts rotation (?) |
| 接力 | jiēlì | relay (race) | momentum handoff between names/sectors | each wave "takes the baton"; failing handoff = 接力断了, the theme dies |
| 发酵 | fājiào | fermentation | a narrative brewing/spreading before the vertical move | 题材发酵中 = the story is still yeasting; the 埋伏 (ambush, see [[trading-slang]]) window |
| 退潮 | tuìcháo | receding tide | end-of-theme phase; capital exits the whole 板块 | the fourth act of the A-share sentiment cycle: 启动 (ignition) → 发酵 → 高潮 (climax) → 退潮 |
| 蹭热点 / 蹭热度 | cèng rèdiǎn / rèdù | rub against the hotspot/heat | hotspot-riding: gluing your project to whatever is trending | the supply side of 概念币 manufacture: rename, tweet, or fork your way into the hot 板块; A-share firms did it with "blockchain" in 2018, memecoin devs do it in minutes |
| 普涨 | pǔzhǎng | universal rise | everything-up broad rally | the opposite of rotation; 山寨全赛道普涨 = full-board altcoin melt-up, the late-cycle blow-off signature |
| 山寨季 | shānzhàijì | mountain-stronghold season | altseason | when rotation goes brrr; punned as 盐寨季/"Salt Season" when it never arrives (?) |
| 主升浪 | zhǔshēnglàng | main rising wave | the primary markup leg | wave-theory register; you 埋伏 during 发酵 to be positioned for the 主升浪 |
| 赚钱效应 / 亏钱效应 | zhuànqián / kuīqián xiàoyìng | money-making / money-losing effect | visible recent P&L that attracts or repels the crowd | the thermodynamic variable of the sentiment cycle; 亏钱效应 at the highs is what triggers 高低切 and 退潮 |
| 复盘 | fùpán | replay the board | post-session review | from chess (go); the daily ritual genre in which all of the above vocabulary lives — CN crypto KOLs publish 复盘 threads exactly like A-share 游资 (hot-money) bloggers |

## The canonical rotation map
CN methodology posts recite a standard bull-market rotation sequence (BlockBeats
meme-trading methodology, similar versions everywhere):

> 大饼 BTC → 以太/公链/L2 → 热点赛道 (whatever the cycle's story is) → Web3
> 基建 → GameFi/元宇宙 → NFT → MEME/动物币 → 牛市结束

The joke is that memecoins are the *terminal* sector — when the 土狗 rotate,
the tide is about to 退潮. The Oct 2025 中文 meme season inverting this (memes
leading, majors flat) was itself read as a structural omen; see
[[meme-season-aftermath]].

Pattern worth noticing: a 概念币 wave needs no consent from the concept. Neither
DeepSeek, nor Musk, nor the HK government issued anything — the 板块 is
manufactured by traders back-fitting an association, then crowned with whatever
龙头 has the best liquidity story. CZ-adjacency plays ([[binance-gravity-well]])
are simply the 概念 grammar with a living oracle attached.

## What transferred, what didn't
- **Transferred:** the full noun system (板块/概念/龙头/补涨/轮动/妖), the
  sentiment-cycle stage model, the 复盘 genre, and the discipline proverbs
  (只干龙头，不干杂毛).
- **Didn't transfer:** the limit-up scaffolding — 涨停板, 首板/连板 (first
  board / consecutive boards), 断板 (board break) stay A-share-only because
  crypto has no price limits; 打板 survives only as a metaphor for chasing
  breakout candles (glossed in [[trading-slang]]).
- **Intensified:** with no T+1 and no closing bell, rotation runs at 24/7 speed;
  CN analysts describe BSC meme sector cycles as compressing the entire
  启动→退潮 arc into days.
- **Flowing back:** the grammar now round-trips — 2025 A-share media traded
  稳定币概念股 ("stablecoin concept stocks," index +21% in June 2025; 四方精创
  +92%) and DeepSeek概念股 (每日互动 +72% in 3 sessions, Feb 2025 (?)) with the
  identical 龙头/补涨/涨停潮 vocabulary. Crypto is just another 概念 to the
  A-share machine that trained everyone.
