# 带单老师 (dàidān lǎoshī) — "The Order-Leading Teacher"

**Literal:** teacher who leads your orders. **Actual:** a scam trading guru who
"mentors" followers into losing trades — often on a fake or kickback exchange.

## Mechanics
The 老师 (teacher) honorific is the con. Chinese education culture grants teachers
automatic deference, and the scam is built on that: an 助理 (assistant) adds you,
you're pulled into a 喊单群 (order-shouting group) where dozens of shills post
profit screenshots and thank 老师 for changing their lives. The teacher calls
leveraged 合约 trades; the group's losses are the operation's revenue — either
through a bucket-shop exchange where your "trades" never hit a real market, or via
volume kickbacks, or by 对冲开单 (taking the opposite side of the room).

The endgame vocabulary is stable across cases: 老师 calls one catastrophic
full-position trade (爆仓, see [[trading-slang]]), the group is dissolved overnight
(解散跑路), and victims discover every other "student" was staff. It's the
industrialized descendant of stock-tip radio scams, and the pipeline that feeds
杀猪盘 romance-investment operations in [[narrative-terms]].

## Usage
- 群里的老师又带单了 — said with either hope (new leek) or dread (old leek)
- 免费带单，稳赚不赔 — "free order-leading, guaranteed profit": the tell itself
