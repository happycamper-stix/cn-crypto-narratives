# Trading Slang (交易黑话)

## Context
Verbs and phrases of the trade. Much of it inherited from A-share stock slang and
HK gambling films, then intensified by crypto. Terms deserving their own atomic
notes are wikilinked.

## Terms

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Origin/mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[ge-jiu-cai\|割韭菜]] | gē jiǔcài | cut leeks | dump on retail | see atomic note |
| 梭哈 | suōhā | "show hand" | all-in | five-card stud via HK gambling films; 一把梭 = YOLO one trade |
| 抄底 | chāodǐ | scoop the bottom | buy the dip | stock slang; 抄底抄在半山腰 = "bought the bottom halfway up the mountain" (knife-catch self-mockery) |
| 拉盘 / 砸盘 | lāpán / zápán | pull / smash the plate | pump / dump | 盘 = the order book |
| 出货 | chūhuò | ship goods | distribution — insiders offloading | 拉高出货 = pump-and-dump |
| 埋伏 | máifú | ambush | position quietly pre-catalyst | military metaphor |
| 冲 | chōng | charge! | ape in | 冲冲冲 chat spam; 冲就完事了 "just ape" |
| 归零 | guīlíng | return to zero | total loss | 归零币 = coin destined for zero |
| 腰斩 | yāozhǎn | cut at the waist | −50% | ancient execution method; 脚踝斩 "ankle cut" = −90% |
| 破发 | pòfā | break issue price | below listing price | IPO slang |
| 打新 | dǎxīn | hit the new | subscribe to launches | A-share IPO-lottery slang |
| 上车 / 下车 | shàngchē / xiàchē | board/exit the bus | enter/exit position | the pump is a departing bus; 还能上车吗? |
| 踏空 | tàkōng | step into emptiness | miss the rally in cash | opposite of 套牢 |
| 套牢 / 解套 | tàoláo / jiětào | trapped / untied | underwater / back to breakeven | stock slang |
| 割肉 | gēròu | cut flesh | capitulate at a loss | cutting off your own flesh to escape |
| 接盘 | jiēpán | catch the plate | be exit liquidity | 接盘侠 "plate-catching knight" = heroic bagholder |
| 喊单 | hǎndān | shout orders | KOL shilling calls | usually precedes dumping on followers |
| 跑路 | pǎolù | run away | rug pull | general scam slang; 卷款跑路 |
| 躺平 / 佛系 | tǎngpíng / fóxì | lie flat / Buddha-style | zen HODL, giving up trading | mainland lifestyle memes |
| 心态崩了 | xīntài bēng le | mentality collapsed | tilted, emotionally rekt | gaming slang |
| 空军 / 多军 | kōngjūn / duōjūn | air force / bull army | shorts / longs | 空 (empty) = short, hence "air force" |
| 插针 | chāzhēn | insert a needle | scam wick / stop hunt | candle wick as needle; exchanges accused of 插针 |
| 爆仓 | bàocāng | position explodes | liquidated | 合约一天，人间一年 "one day of futures = a year of life" |
| 搬砖 | bānzhuān | move bricks | cross-exchange arbitrage | menial-labor metaphor |
| 老鼠仓 | lǎoshǔcāng | rat warehouse | insider front-run position | fund-scandal slang |
| 对敲 | duìqiāo | mutual knocking | wash trading | exchange fake-volume slang |
