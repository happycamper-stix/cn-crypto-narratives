# K线 / TA Chart-Reading Slang (K线黑话)

## Context
CN crypto discourse is saturated with A-share-derived chartism: every 带单老师
([[dai-dan-lao-shi]]) narrates in this register, Odaily/AICoin run daily 行情分析
columns written entirely in it, and it is productive enough to mint memecoins
(人生K线, Jan 2026 — see [[meme-season-aftermath]]). The register imports three
older layers at once: Japanese rice-trader candlestick vocabulary (via 1990s
A-share manuals), A-share retail-vs-庄家 adversarial framing, and casino language
(筹码 "chips" — see [[gambling-film-register]]).

**Name etymology**: K线 (K-xiàn, "K-line") is *not* from "candle" or "curve" —
a folk etymology CN textbooks repeat. The K transliterates Japanese 罫線
(*keisen*, "ruled/grid line"), the Japanese name for the chart (The Paper,
2022-02-05). HK/TW instead say 阴阳烛 (yīnyángzhú, "yin-yang candles") or
蜡烛图 (làzhútú, "candle chart"); Taiwan says K棒 (K-bàng, "K-bar").
Candlesticks trace to Edo-era rice trader 本间宗久 (Honma Munehisa) of Sakata,
hence the older name 酒田战法 ("Sakata tactics").

**The color trap**: mainland charts are 红涨绿跌 — red means UP, green means
DOWN, inverted from Western convention. This is load-bearing for anyone reading
Chinese screenshots; full treatment in [[hong-zhang-lv-die]]. Safest decoding
habit: trust 阴/阳 words (convention-independent) over 红/绿 words
(convention-dependent). 收阳 = closed up, always; 收红 = closed up only on a
red-up chart.

## Candle anatomy and single-candle forms

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K线 | K-xiàn | K-line | candlestick; the chart itself | 看K线 = "do TA"; 日K/周K/月K = daily/weekly/monthly candles |
| 阳线 / 阴线 | yángxiàn / yīnxiàn | yang line / yin line | up-close candle / down-close candle | yin-yang cosmology, predates color; 收阳/收阴 = closed up/down (unambiguous) |
| 大阳线 / 大阴线 | dà yángxiàn / dà yīnxiàn | big yang/yin line | big-bodied up/down candle | 拉出大阳线 = "printed a big green (CN: red) candle" |
| 光头光脚 | guāngtóu guāngjiǎo | bald head, bare feet | marubozu — no upper/lower shadow | 光头阳线 = no upper wick; full form 光头光脚大阳线 |
| 上影线 / 下影线 | shàngyǐngxiàn / xiàyǐngxiàn | upper/lower shadow line | upper/lower wick | long 上影线 = selling pressure above; cf. 插针 in [[trading-slang]] |
| 十字星 | shízìxīng | cross star | doji | read as 变盘信号 (regime-change signal); 高位十字星 = topping warning |
| 锤子线 | chuízixiàn | hammer line | hammer (bottom reversal) | long lower wick after a decline |
| 上吊线 | shàngdiàoxiàn | hanging line | hanging man (top reversal) | literally the hanged-man rope; same shape as hammer but at highs |
| 射击之星 | shèjī zhī xīng | shooting star | shooting star (top reversal) | long upper wick at highs |
| 金针探底 | jīnzhēn tàndǐ | golden needle probes the bottom | long lower wick marking a bottom | the wick as an acupuncture needle that finds support; bullish |
| 仙人指路 | xiānrén zhǐlù | the immortal points the way | long upper-wick probe candle read as 主力 testing overhead supply before a real pump | Daoist-immortal imagery; a.k.a. 宝剑出鞘 "sword leaves the scabbard"; bullish *if* you trust the 庄家 |
| 天地针 | tiāndìzhēn | heaven-and-earth needle | full-range double scam wick | see [[trading-slang]]; crypto-perp intensification of wick language |

## Multi-candle patterns and crosses

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 金叉 / 死叉 | jīnchā / sǐchā | golden fork / death fork | golden cross / death cross | any bullish/bearish line crossover (MACD, KDJ, MAs); 零轴下方金叉 = golden cross below the zero line — staple of Odaily guest analysis (?) |
| 顶背离 / 底背离 | dǐng bèilí / dǐ bèilí | top/bottom deviation | bearish/bullish divergence | price makes new high/low, indicator doesn't; the workhorse reversal argument in CN columns |
| 红三兵 | hóng sān bīng | three red soldiers | three white soldiers (bullish) | "red" because CN up-candles are red — the pattern name itself encodes [[hong-zhang-lv-die]] |
| 三只乌鸦 | sān zhī wūyā | three crows | three black crows (bearish) | three consecutive 阴线 at a top |
| 早晨之星 / 黄昏之星 | zǎochén zhī xīng / huánghūn zhī xīng | morning star / evening star | 3-candle bottom / top reversal | direct calques of the Japanese names |
| 乌云盖顶 | wūyún gàidǐng | dark clouds cover the peak | dark cloud cover (bearish engulfing-ish) | vivid weather imagery, cf. 瀑布 in [[trading-slang]] |
| 多方炮 | duōfāngpào | bull-side cannon | 两阳夹一阴 — two up candles sandwiching one down | when follow-through comes, 多方开炮 "the bulls open fire"; inverse 空方炮; military register — [[martial-military-metaphors]] |
| 空中加油 | kōngzhōng jiāyóu | mid-air refueling | high-position washout candle (gap-up, long upper wick, volume spike) mid-uptrend, read as a pause before continuation | fighter-jet imagery; A-share bull-market classic migrated wholesale into 币圈 commentary |
| 断头铡刀 | duàntóu zhádāo | head-chopping guillotine | one huge 阴线 slicing down through several MAs at once | the 铡刀 is Judge Bao's opera guillotine; unambiguous top signal |
| M头 / W底 | M-tóu / W-dǐ | M-top / W-bottom | double top / double bottom | letter-shape naming; TW crypto-ed sites still teach it this way (幣修學分, 2026-02-04) |
| 头肩顶 / 头肩底 | tóujiāndǐng / tóujiāndǐ | head-shoulder top/bottom | head and shoulders (top/inverse) | standard calque |
| 箱体震荡 | xiāngtǐ zhèndàng | box-body oscillation | range-bound chop between defined levels | 箱体 = the box; cf. 横盘 in [[trading-slang]] |
| 画门 | huàmén | drawing a door | vertical dump + vertical reclaim (or the reverse) tracing a door frame; by extension, blatant 庄家-drawn price action | crypto-native; canonized in the EOS 庄家 era — 老庄画门 "the old banker paints a door" (AICoin column, "每一次暴涨前都会形成画门行情", 2019-05-16 (?)) |

## The price-analysis column register (levels and verbs)
The formulaic vocabulary of every CN 行情分析 post — e.g. "交易时刻: BTC在8.8万和
9万区间存在强支撑" (PANews 交易时刻 column via 腾讯新闻, 2026-01-12):

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 支撑位 | zhīchēngwèi | support position | support level |
| 压力位 / 阻力位 | yālìwèi / zǔlìwèi | pressure/resistance position | resistance level |
| 站上 / 站稳 | zhànshàng / zhànwěn | stand on / stand firm | break above / hold above a level |
| 回踩 | huícǎi | step back on | pull back to retest support; 回踩确认 = successful retest |
| 破位 | pòwèi | break position | lose support; breakdown |
| 承压 | chéngyā | bear pressure | rejected at / capped by resistance |
| 企稳 | qǐwěn | stand stable | stabilize, stop falling |
| 变盘 | biànpán | change the plate | imminent regime change (direction unresolved); 变盘在即 |
| 突破 / 假突破 | tūpò / jiǎ tūpò | breakthrough / false breakthrough | breakout / fakeout |
| 挖坑 | wākēng | dig a pit | engineered washout dip below support before markup |
| 均线 | jūnxiàn | average line | moving average; 5日线/60日线 etc.; 季线=60d, 半年线=120d, 年线=250d |
| 多头排列 / 空头排列 | duōtóu páiliè / kōngtóu páiliè | bull/bear formation | MAs stacked bullishly/bearishly (military "formation" — [[martial-military-metaphors]]) |
| 牛熊分界线 | niú-xióng fēnjièxiàn | bull-bear boundary line | the MA that "decides" the cycle: 年线 in A-shares; in BTC discourse usually the 200-week or 200-day MA (?) |
| 布林带 | bùlíndài | Bolin belt | Bollinger Bands ("布林" transliterates Bollinger) |
| 裸K | luǒ-K | naked K | price action trading, no indicators |

## Volume register (量能)

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 放量 / 缩量 | fàngliàng / suōliàng | release/shrink volume | volume expansion / contraction; 放量突破 = high-volume breakout, 缩量回调 = low-volume pullback (healthy) |
| 天量天价 | tiānliàng tiānjià | heaven volume, heaven price | record volume marks the top |
| 地量地价 | dìliàng dìjià | earth volume, earth price | dead volume marks the bottom |
| 量价背离 | liàngjià bèilí | volume-price deviation | volume not confirming price; reversal warning |
| 量在价先 | liàng zài jià xiān | volume precedes price | the axiom licensing all of the above |

Proverb: 新手看价，老手看量，高手看势 — "novices watch price, veterans watch
volume, masters watch the trend."

## The deception cluster: charts as things 庄家 draw
CN chartism is unusually cynical: the default assumption is that patterns are
*painted* by the 庄家 (see [[community-roles]]) precisely because retail reads
them. The proverb K线是庄家画出来的 ("K-lines are drawn by the banker") coexists
with obsessive K-line reading — cope and creed at once ([[irony-cope-register]]).

| Term | Pinyin | Literal | Actual meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 骗线 | piànxiàn | deception line | a chart pattern deliberately painted to trap TA readers (Baidu Baike codifies it as a 庄家 technique) |
| 诱多 / 诱空 | yòuduō / yòukōng | lure the bulls / lure the shorts | bull trap / bear trap |
| 洗盘 | xǐpán | wash the plate | shakeout of weak hands before markup; the eternal headline question 洗盘还是出货? "shakeout or distribution?" |
| 吸筹 | xīchóu | suck in chips | quiet accumulation by the 主力/庄家 |
| 筹码 | chóumǎ | casino chips | float/token supply as poker chips; the master metaphor ([[gambling-film-register]]) |
| 筹码分布 | chóumǎ fēnbù | chip distribution | volume-at-price histogram — a standard indicator in CN stock software (通达信/同花顺 lineage) largely unknown to Western retail; 筹码峰 = chip peak (dense supply zone), 获利盘/套牢盘 = in-profit vs trapped supply |
| 控盘 | kòngpán | control the plate | (of a 庄家) to fully control a coin's float; 高控盘 = tightly held |
| 主力 | zhǔlì | main force | "the operator" — polite synonym for 庄家; military register |

## Schools and authority costumes
TA fluency is the KOL's credential costume ([[kol-shilling-ecosystem]]); the
K线教主 "K-line cult leader" KOL persona is tiered in [[cn-ct-kol-directory]].
- **缠论** (Chánlùn, "Entanglement Theory") — indigenous Chinese TA school from
  the 缠中说禅 blog series 教你炒股票 ("Teaching You to Trade Stocks", first post
  2006-06-07; author identified as 李彪, d. 2008). Its jargon — 中枢 (zhōngshū,
  "pivot center"), 笔 (bǐ, "stroke"), 线段 (xiànduàn, "segment"), 背驰 (bèichí,
  Chan's own word for divergence), 一买/二买/三买 (first/second/third buy points)
  — circulates heavily in CN crypto trading groups; a marker of "serious
  homework" the way Wyckoff is on Western CT.
- **江恩** (Jiāng'ēn, Gann) and **波浪理论** (bōlàng lǐlùn, Elliott Wave, with
  数浪 "wave counting") — the imported classics, staple 带单 teaching material.
- **分时图** (fēnshítú, "time-share chart") — the intraday line chart (white
  price line + yellow average-cost line) that is the default view in A-share
  apps; CN crypto apps (AICoin, exchange apps) kept it as a tab. A CN screenshot
  showing 分时图 rather than candles is itself a tell of A-share-trained habits.

## Culture: the chart as fate
人生K线 (rénshēng K-xiàn, "life K-line") — the Jan 2026 BSC memecoin
([[meme-season-aftermath]]) — works because "your life as a candlestick chart"
was already a cliché of CN trading culture: motivational essays about 人生的K线,
腰斩 lives and 反弹 comebacks. The K线 register is not just analysis; it is a
fatalist idiom for narrating luck, the same slot 赌命 language occupies in
[[gambling-film-register]].

## Decoding heuristics for analysts
1. **Never read CN chart colors without checking the axis** — see
   [[hong-zhang-lv-die]]. Crypto apps default to green-up even in Chinese, but
   mainland users habitually flip them to 红涨绿跌 in settings (Binance and OKX
   both offer the toggle), so KOL screenshots come in *both* conventions.
2. 阴/阳 vocabulary is convention-proof; 红/绿 vocabulary is not.
3. When a pattern name sounds like opera/wuxia (断头铡刀, 仙人指路), it is
   A-share-manual heritage; when it sounds like aviation or artillery
   (空中加油, 多方炮), likewise. Purely crypto-native chart slang is rarer and
   usually 庄家-cynical (画门, 插针, 天地针).
4. A CN analysis post is often a *social* document: 骗线/诱多 talk signals
   "I know the 庄家 is watching us read this" — adversarial epistemology baked
   into the genre.
