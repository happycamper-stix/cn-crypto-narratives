# 大饼 (dàbǐng) — "Big Flatbread"

**Literal:** big pancake/flatbread. **Actual:** Bitcoin.

## Origin
BTC's round gold-coin logo looks like a 饼; partly censorship-evasion euphemism
after the 2017 mainland crackdown (writing 大饼 instead of 比特币 dodged keyword
filters). "Eating the flatbread" = holding BTC; 大饼yyds = "BTC is the GOAT."

## Family
- 二饼 — "second flatbread": ETH (which also has the racier nickname [[da-yi-tai]])
- The food-naming pattern extends across majors: 柚子 (pomelo, EOS), 辣条 (spicy
  snack, LTC) — see [[coin-nicknames]]
