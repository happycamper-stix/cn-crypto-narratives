# 冻卡 (dòngkǎ) — "Frozen Card"

**Literal:** frozen (bank) card. **Actual:** having your bank account frozen by
police after an OTC crypto sale routed you tainted money.

## Mechanics
With exchanges banned from touching CNY, the mainland off-ramp is peer-to-peer OTC:
you sell USDT ("出U") to a stranger who wires you yuan. If that yuan came from
telecom fraud, gambling, or 杀猪盘 proceeds (see [[narrative-terms]]), the
anti-fraud system freezes every downstream account it touched — yours included.
Freezes run 3 days to 6 months+, sometimes requiring a trip to a police station in
a distant province to plead your innocence.

This spawned an entire risk-management folk practice: 冻卡潮 ("freeze waves"),
黑U (tainted USDT), dedicated cards used only for crypto, pricing in a premium for
"clean" fiat, and the 卡农 (card farmers) and U商 (OTC desks) ecosystem in
[[community-roles]]. 冻卡 is *the* structural fear that separates the CN trading
experience from the Western one: profit isn't real until 出金 survives.

## Usage
- 出金冻卡了 — "withdrew and got frozen"
- 求推荐不冻卡的U商 — perennial group-chat plea
