# 无币区块链 (wúbì qūkuàiliàn) — "Coinless Blockchain"

**Literal:** without-coin blockchain. **Actual:** blockchain with the tradable
token surgically removed — the only kind of chain the mainland state sanctions,
and therefore the load-bearing concept of the whole "链 good, 币 bad" policy
divide.

## Origin
Crystallized in 2018 policy/academic discourse as regulators looked for a way
to keep the technology while banning the asset. The canonical statement is the
PBoC research line (徐忠, then head of the PBoC research bureau, with 邹传伟,
Nov 2018 working paper "区块链能做什么、不能做什么" — "what blockchain can and
cannot do"): the useful applications are permissioned, real-world-anchored,
and do not need a speculative token. Exact first coiner of the four-character
phrase unclear (?). Xi's Oct 24, 2019 Politburo speech elevating 区块链 to a
national core technology — followed within days by People's Daily's
"区块链技术创新不等于炒作虚拟货币" corrective — made 无币区块链 the official
investable reading of "the state supports blockchain."

## Institutional expressions
- **联盟链** (liánméngliàn, consortium chains): permissioned chains for
  courts (司法链), e-invoices, supply chains — the 链圈's day job.
- **BSN** (区块链服务网络, launched 2020) and its "开放联盟链": global public
  chains re-hosted for China with gas paid in RMB and no tradable token —
  无币 as infrastructure policy.
- **数字藏品** (shùzì cángpǐn, "digital collectibles"): NFTs admitted by
  amputation — real-name, RMB-priced, no secondary market, gifting (转赠)
  only. The renaming is the regulation.

## Community rejoinder
The 币圈 mocks the concept as **太监链** (tàijiàn liàn, "eunuch chain") — a
chain with its incentive organ removed: no token, no reason for anyone to run
a node or care. The sneer encodes the 链圈/币圈 status war
([[suffix-morphology]]): 链圈 gets legitimacy without upside; 币圈 gets upside
without legitimacy.

## Usage
"国内只能做无币区块链" = "onshore you can only build the coinless kind" — the
one-line explanation for why every CN-founded token project incorporates
offshore while its tech subsidiary stays onshore doing 联盟链 contracts.
