# 韭菜 (jiǔcài) — "Leeks"

**Literal:** leek/garlic chives. **Actual:** retail investors as an endlessly
harvestable class.

## Why leeks
Chives regrow after cutting — you can harvest the same patch forever. Retail keeps
coming back to be cut. Inherited from A-share stock slang, now the root metaphor of
the entire CN crypto lexicon (see [[food-metaphors]]).

## Family
- [[ge-jiu-cai]] 割韭菜 — "cutting leeks", the harvesting act
- 老韭菜 — "old leek": cycle-scarred veteran, worn as a badge of honor
- 新韭菜 / 小白 — newbie
- 韭菜盒子 — "leek pastry": a container of doomed retail (a portfolio or victim group chat)
- 孙割 — Justin Sun's nickname, "Sun the Cutter" (see [[community-roles]])

## Usage
Self-deprecating self-identifier: Chinese retail calls *itself* 韭菜, unlike English
"exit liquidity" which is usually pointed at others.
