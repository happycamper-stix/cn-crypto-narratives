# The Chinese KOL / 带单 Ecosystem: How Narratives Propagate in CN Channels

## Context
Western crypto narratives propagate in public (CT threads, Discord). Chinese
narratives propagate in *closed* group chats — WeChat 微信群, Telegram 电报群,
QQ groups — mediated by KOLs whose business model is built on that opacity.
This changes how narratives feel: by the time a story is public, insiders have
positioned.

## Core roles and verbs
- **喊单** (hǎndān, "shout orders") — publicly calling a trade/coin. The
  baseline KOL act; "跟单" (gēndān) is following the call.
- **带单** (dàidān, "lead orders") — running a paid group where the 老师
  (lǎoshī, "teacher") trades live and members copy. The "teacher" honorific is
  load-bearing: it imports tutoring-culture deference into gambling.
- **带单老师 / 老师** — often a front for the counterparty: classic 杀猪盘-style
  setups have the teacher's brokerage as the bucket shop.
- **社区** (shèqū, "community") — euphemism for a shill group; 社区长/群主 run
  it, 水军 (shuǐjūn, "water army" = paid posters) fill it.
- **KOL轮** ("KOL round") — 2024–25 innovation: projects allocate discounted
  tokens to dozens of KOLs in lieu of marketing spend. Sina/ChainCatcher
  coverage frames the result bluntly: followers "buy the KOL's exit
  liquidity" — the call *is* the unlock.

## The funnel
1. Project pays or allocates to KOLs (X/Twitter 中文区, YouTube, 抖音-adjacent).
2. KOLs post 研报-style threads (pseudo research) + 喊单.
3. Free TG/WeChat groups amplify; the real action is in paid inner groups
   (内部群, "insider group") sold with 财富密码 (cáifù mìmǎ, "wealth code")
   language.
4. 割韭菜 → group is disbanded (解散群) or renamed; KOL reputation is
  laundered by the next meta. Rug on followers = 割粉丝 ("harvesting fans").

## CN vs Western CT propagation differences
- **Closed vs open**: information asymmetry is the product; screenshots of
  group calls (截图) circulate as second-hand alpha.
- **Speed and swarm**: a 群 can coordinate a buy within minutes — this is why
  CZ tweets convert to tickers faster in the CN meta ([[binance-gravity-well]]).
- **Teacher-student framing** vs Western "degen equals" framing — deference
  makes 带单 scams stickier.
- **Platform churn**: WeChat crackdowns push groups to Telegram; the 2019 WeChat
  ban on crypto accounts made TG the default narrative venue for the diaspora.
- **The "clean" tier**: 纯干货/清流 KOLs (analysis-only, no paid calls) exist
  and are rank-listed by Feixiaohao/Bitget precisely because the default
  assumption is that everyone else is compromised.
