# Chinese Memecoin Naming Meta (2024–2026)

## Context
Oct 2025 brought the first large-scale export of Chinese internet subculture as
financial assets: the BNB Chain "中文 meme season." CZ posts celebrating BSC meme
activity kicked it off; daily DEX volume passed $6B and Four.meme (BSC launchpad —
its own name riffs on CZ's "4" tweet-code, see below) briefly out-earned pump.fun.

## Naming conventions observed

**Blessing-formula (祝福体).** Repurpose brand language as a benediction with the
"___人生" ("-life") suffix, from 港股人生 ("H-share life" = striking it rich):
- **币安人生** ("Binance Life") — flagship: launched Oct 4 2025 on BSC, ~$524M mcap
  in 4 days after He Yi (一姐) replied 祝你币安人生 and CZ chimed in.
- Clones: **Base人生** on Base, **索拉拉** (Solala — cutesy reduplication of
  Solana's transliteration) on Solana.

**CZ-adjacency.** Anything CZ mentions becomes a ticker within minutes:
- **BROCCOLI** (Feb 2025) — CZ revealed his dog's name; ~20,000 broccoli tokens in
  days; the leader pumped then fell >75%.
- **TST** — a token from a Four.meme *tutorial video* CZ shared; aped to a huge cap,
  then crashed; CZ donated BNB to victims. Peak "attention = asset, meaning optional."
- **$4** — tokenization of CZ's numbered-tweet shorthand for "ignore FUD" (also why
  Four.meme leans into the otherwise-unlucky 4; see [[number-slang]]).

**Crude-directness / swagger.** 我踏马来了 ("I f***ing arrived") — profane
arrival-announcement as ticker; 老子 (Lǎozǐ) — doubles as the philosopher and the
arrogant first-person "I, your daddy."

**Aphorism & cuteness families.** 慢慢变富-style ("get rich slowly") aphorism coins;
snack/food names (包子/BAO — reads well in English too); panda nationalism-lite.

## Why Chinese-language tickers work (TechFlow's analysis)
Names optimize for 视觉简洁 (visual simplicity), 情绪饱和 (emotional saturation),
语义模糊 (semantic ambiguity); no need to translate the joke for Western audiences.
Psychological driver: 改变命运的门票 — "a ticket to change one's destiny" in a
low-growth economy.
