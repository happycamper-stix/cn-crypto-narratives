# HK / TW / Mainland: One Language, Three Crypto Worlds

## Context
"Chinese crypto" is not one market. Mainland (banned but omnipresent via VPN),
Hong Kong (licensed and courting Web3), and Taiwan (grey-zone retail market)
run different regulatory regimes, different platforms, and noticeably different
vocabulary. Terms travel between them with tell-tale accents.

## Hong Kong: the 香港Web3 narrative
Post-FTX, HK pivoted: the Oct 2022 policy statement and June 2023 VASP
licensing regime let SFC-licensed exchanges (HashKey, OSL) serve retail. The
**香港Web3** narrative — HK as the compliant Chinese-language on-ramp and
sandbox Beijing watches from a distance ("一国两制 for crypto") — pumped every
"Hong Kong concept coin" (香港概念币, e.g. CFX as the only 合规 mainland-linked
chain) in H1 2023. Reality check came fast: **JPEX** (Sept 2023, ~HK$1.6B, HK's
biggest crypto fraud) — an unlicensed exchange sold by influencers and OTC
shops (找换店) — became the license regime's test case and taught the market
the phrase 无牌平台 (unlicensed platform). 2024–25: spot ETFs, stablecoin
ordinance (Aug 2025). The narrative survives but is now institutional, not
retail-degen.

## Taiwan
No ban; AML-registration regime moving toward dedicated legislation. Retail
culture centers on PTT/Dcard forums, 交易所 MAX/BitoPro, and TG groups; ACE
exchange fraud case (2024) is the local JPEX. TW slang skews translated-Western
("上車" for getting in — shared with HK bus metaphor) and imports mainland
terms with quotation-mark irony.

## Terminology accents (spotting the speaker)
- **Meme coin**: mainland says **MEME币** or 迷因币 rarely; TW/HK prefer
  **迷因币** (míyīn bì — 迷因 is the standard TW translation of "meme");
  mainland media often just writes "Meme" in Latin letters.
- **Simplified vs traditional characters** — first-glance origin signal
  (韭菜 vs 韭菜 identical, but 网络/網路, 数字资产/數位資產 differ; TW says
  數位, HK 數碼).
- **虚拟资产** (virtual assets) is HK regulator-speak (SFC); mainland official
  documents say 虚拟货币 (virtual currency, always pejoratively); industry says
  加密货币/Web3.
- **出金/入金** (fiat off/on-ramp) universal, but mainland adds the OTC dialect
  (U商, "USDT merchants"; 冻卡, "frozen bank card" — a uniquely mainland fear,
  since OTC counterparties may be laundering scam money, see
  [[pig-butchering-dark-lexicon]]).

## Why divergence matters for narratives
Regulatory arbitrage is itself a narrative engine: each HK policy statement is
traded like a CZ tweet, and mainland-facing KOLs launder "the state is secretly
pro-crypto" hopium through HK news. Meanwhile mainland's ban keeps its retail
in offshore/DEX venues — which is why mainland-driven manias (铭文, BSC memes)
happen on-chain, not on licensed exchanges.
