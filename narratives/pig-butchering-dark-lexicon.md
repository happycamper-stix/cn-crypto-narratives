# 杀猪盘 and the Scam-Compound Lexicon

## Context
The darkest branch of Chinese crypto vocabulary comes from the Southeast Asian
scam-compound economy. Its jargon leaked into mainstream slang so thoroughly
that ordinary traders now joke in trafficker vocabulary — a case study in how
crime dialect feeds back into the mother tongue.

## Core terms
- **杀猪盘** (shāzhūpán, "pig-butchering plate/scheme") — romance-investment
  fraud: the scammer 养猪 ("raises the pig" — grooming the victim, the 猪) then
  杀猪 ("slaughters") via a fake trading platform. Coined in Chinese scam-world
  argot ~2018; now a loanword in English ("pig butchering") and FBI reports.
- **园区** (yuánqū, "park/industrial zone") — the euphemism for scam compounds
  in Myanmar (缅北, "northern Myanmar" — KK园区 the most notorious), Cambodia
  (西港, Sihanoukville), Laos. "被骗到园区" ("tricked into a park") = trafficked.
- **狗推** (gǒutuī, "dog-pusher") — compound slang for the front-line scam
  chatters, often themselves trafficked; self-deprecating and dehumanizing at
  once. **猪仔** ("piglets") = trafficked workers sold between compounds.
- **跑分** (pǎofēn, "point-running") — renting your bank/crypto accounts to
  launder scam proceeds; the retail-facing edge of the machine.
- **U商** (U-shāng, "USDT merchant") — OTC tether dealers; the settlement rail
  of the entire economy (Tether/OKX/DOJ froze $225M linked to one syndicate in
  2023; the 2025 Prince Group/Chen Zhi case seized ~127k BTC). Consequence for
  civilians: **冻卡** (dòngkǎ, "frozen card") — your bank card frozen because an
  OTC counterparty passed you tainted funds; whole guides exist on 冻卡-safe
  cashing out. 黑U ("black USDT") = tainted tether.

## Feedback into mainstream slang
- 杀猪盘 generalized: any project/KOL group that grooms then dumps on followers
  is called a 杀猪盘 — 带单老师 groups especially ([[kol-shilling-ecosystem]]).
  "我成了猪" ("I became the pig") = I got groomed-and-dumped.
- 缅北 became a dark meme: "再亏就去缅北了" ("one more loss and it's off to
  Myanmar") — gallows-humor for being so broke you'd fall for a job scam;
  电诈 (telecom fraud) discourse saturates mainland media (and the 2023 hit
  film 孤注一掷 *No More Bets* cemented compound imagery in pop culture).
- 噶腰子 ("harvesting kidneys" — organ-theft rumors about compounds) is now
  generic slang for being brutally exploited, crypto or not.
- The state's anti-fraud campaign vocabulary (反诈, National Anti-Fraud Center
  app) frames *all* crypto as adjacent to 电诈 — reinforcing the official
  virtual-currency-equals-crime narrative and, perversely, pushing legitimate
  users deeper into offshore channels ([[hk-tw-mainland-divergence]]).

## Why it matters for decoding
When a CN trader says 杀猪盘, 狗推, or jokes about 缅北, they're borrowing from
a real economy measured in tens of billions of dollars and trafficked lives.
The humor is a coping register; the underlying referent is documented by
Chainalysis, the DOJ, and Wu Blockchain's compound reporting.
