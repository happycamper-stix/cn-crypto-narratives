# 铭文 Mania: Why Inscriptions Were a Chinese-Retail Narrative

## Context
**铭文** (míngwén, "inscription" — classically, text engraved on bronze or stone)
is the Chinese term for Bitcoin Ordinals inscriptions. BRC-20 launched March 8,
2023 (anonymous dev domo); the first token **ORDI** ran two hype waves (May–June
and Oct–Nov 2023). Western CT was split ("spam on Bitcoin"); Chinese-language
crypto media (TechFlow's "铭文、Ordinals 和 BRC-20，再次让比特币变得有趣") and
communities embraced it hardest — the first narrative since the 2021 expulsions
where CN retail visibly set the price.

## Why it resonated culturally
- **公平发射** (gōngpíng fāshè, "fair launch"): no VC, no presale, no team
  allocation — everyone mints at cost. After years of feeling like exit
  liquidity for 西方 VC coins, "no 庄家 (zhuāngjiā, banker/whale)" was the
  pitch. 铭文 was framed as the anti-割韭菜 asset.
- **打铭文** (dǎ míngwén, "hitting/striking inscriptions") — minting as
  grindable piecework. 打 is the same verb as in 打工 (working a job): minting
  was literally talked about as labor with a lottery attached, perfectly suited
  to studio/工作室 culture ([[lumao-industrial-complex]]).
- **Digital-artifact classicism**: 铭文 evokes bronze-age engravings; "carving
  text into digital gold" flattered Chinese cultural affinity for calligraphy,
  seals, and permanence — the narrative wrote itself in a way "JSON in a
  witness field" never could in English.
- **Mint-rush mechanics** matched the CN meta: speed, gas wars, Telegram/WeChat
  group coordination, tooling (Unisat wallet — itself CN-built) — the same
  swarm skills honed in IEO and whitelist eras.

## Anatomy of the frenzy
Late 2023: text inscriptions passed 10M+, ORDI listed on Binance (Nov 7, 2023 —
Binance listing as narrative validation, see [[binance-gravity-well]]), and the
mania spread to every chain as **EVM 铭文** — minters spam-inscribed on
Polygon, Avalanche, even halting several chains with pure calldata mints. 打新
("hitting new [mints]") tutorials and 省钱教程 (gas-saving guides) flooded
CN media. Derivative vocabulary: **符文** (fúwén, "runes") for the 2024 Runes
protocol; 图腾/domain metas followed.

## The unwind
Most BRC-20s round-tripped in 2024; ORDI fell >90% from highs. But the era's
vocabulary survived: 公平发射 is now a standard launchpad marketing claim
(pump.fun/Four.meme inherit the framing), and 打 as the minting/sniping verb
carried straight into 打狗 ("hitting dogs" = sniping meme coins) in the 2025
meme season.
