# Patriotic Coin Narratives: 国产币, 民族币, and Nationalism as a Shill

## Context
Nationalism is one of the oldest and most durable framings in Chinese crypto
shilling — from legitimate-ish "domestic public chain" pride to outright MLM
fraud. Understanding the register matters: the same words appear in VC decks and
in police fraud bulletins.

## The legitimate(ish) layer: 国产公链
**国产** (guóchǎn, "domestically produced") — 2017's 国产公链 wave sold NEO
(小蚁), Qtum (量子链), Tron (波场), later Conflux (树图) as "China's Ethereum."
Pitch: regulatory tailwind ("the state supports 区块链 blockchain, just not
炒币 speculation" — especially after Xi's Oct 2019 blockchain speech, which
pumped every 国产 ticker). The 2019 speech front-ran a whole 炒作 (chǎozuò,
hype) cycle of "policy-concept coins."

## The scam layer: 传销币 pitch mechanics
**传销币** (chuánxiāo bì, MLM coin; euphemism **CX币**) and 资金盘 recruiters
lean on patriotic scripts documented in PRC anti-fraud bulletins:
- **"中国人自己的币"** ("a coin of the Chinese people's own") — buying is framed
  as patriotic duty; skepticism as helping foreign finance suppress China.
- **民族资产解冻** (mínzú zīchǎn jiědòng, "unfreezing national assets") — a
  decades-old fraud genre (secret KMT/imperial treasure funds to be "unlocked"
  with your deposit) re-skinned with tokens; "中国梦" (China Dream) project
  names ride official slogan language.
- **国家扶持/央行背书** claims — fake state endorsement, forged 红头文件
  (red-header official documents), sometimes staged photos with officials.
- Targets skew older/lower-tier cities: 下乡洗脑 ("go down to the countryside
  to brainwash") roadshows, 团队长 (tuánzhǎng, team leaders) recruiting via
  WeChat, returns promised in 静态/动态收益 ("static/dynamic yield" — MLM
  jargon for hold-yield vs recruit-yield).

Notorious specimens: 五行币 (Wǔxíng Coin, 2016–17, "Five Elements Coin" with
gold-plated Xi-era iconography), PlusToken's "national-champion wallet" framing,
and endless 某某链 regional-government-cosplay chains.

## Nationalism-lite in meme land
The 2025–26 meme season recycles the energy ironically: panda coins, 中华
tickers, "Chinese people's own meme season" discourse around BSC — pride framing
without the MLM machinery, closer to identity-meme than fraud, though the
line blurs fast when KOLs shill ([[kol-shilling-ecosystem]]). TST/币安人生-era
commentary explicitly celebrated 中文 tickers beating English ones as cultural
export ([[launchpad-naming-meta]]).

## Decoder heuristics
If a pitch contains 国家/民族 + guaranteed yield + recruitment tiers, it's a
传销币 script. Genuine projects brag about tech and listings; scams brag about
the motherland and 静态收益.
